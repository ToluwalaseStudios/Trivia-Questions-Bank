Q: The sum of the coefficients of all species (reactants and products) in the balanced equation for the reaction between aqueous mixture of lead (II) nitrate and sodium phosphate is
A) 4
B) 6
C) 10
D) 12
ANSWER: D
EXPLANATION: 3Pb(NO₃)₂ + 2Na₃PO₄ → Pb₃(PO₄)₂ + 6NaNO₃. Coefficients: 3+2+1+6 = 12.

Q: The volume occupied by a certain mass of hydrogen gas collected over water at 28°C and 769 mmHg was 35 cm³. What would be the volume in cm³ of the dry gas at S.T.P if the saturated vapour pressure of water at 28°C is 13.5 mmHg?
A) 31.6
B) 31.0
C) 17.3
D) 35.0
ANSWER: A
EXPLANATION: Pressure of dry gas = 769 – 13.5 = 755.5 mmHg. Using combined gas law to convert to STP (273 K, 760 mmHg) gives approximately 31.6 cm³.

Q: Which of the following decreases across the period?
A) Electronegativity
B) Atomic radius
C) Ionization energy
D) Electron affinity
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: Atomic radius decreases across a period because of increasing nuclear charge pulling the electrons closer.

Q: The decay series of ²³⁸U is abbreviated as follows: ²³⁸U → ... → ²⁰⁶Pb. Identify both the number and types of radiation represented by the letters Q, R respectively.
A) 2-beta and 2-neutron and 2-gamma
B) 3-alpha, 2-beta, and 2-neutron
C) 3-beta, 2-alpha and 3-gamma
D) 2-alpha, 2-beta and 3-neutron
ANSWER: (Corrected standard) The series from ²³⁸U to ²⁰⁶Pb involves 8 alpha and 6 beta decays.

Q: An ion Xⁿ⁺ has 14 electrons. How many unpaired electrons are there in the neutral atom of X?
A) 4
B) 3
C) 2
D) 1
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: Neutral atom has 14 + n electrons. For common cases (e.g. Si or similar) the number of unpaired electrons is typically 2 in the outer shell.

Q: Sodium chloride can be separated from rock salt by first adding water to the mixture to dissolve the sodium chloride. The further separation stages are
A) filtration followed by decantation
B) evaporation followed by filtration
C) distillation followed by decantation
D) filtration followed by evaporation
ANSWER: D
EXPLANATION: After dissolving, insoluble impurities are removed by filtration, then the filtrate is evaporated to obtain pure NaCl crystals.

Q: Which of the following sets of oxides is neutral?
A) ZnO and NO
B) Na₂O and CO
C) SO₂ and N₂O
D) CO and N₂O
ANSWER: D
EXPLANATION: CO and N₂O are classic neutral oxides; they do not react with acids or bases.

Q: Which of these is sp² hybridized?
A) H₂O
B) BF₃
C) NH₃
D) CH₄
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: BF₃ has three bonding pairs and no lone pairs → trigonal planar → sp² hybridisation.

Q: A chemical reaction proceeds with increase in both enthalpy and entropy. This reaction will be spontaneous if
A) ΔH > TΔS
B) ΔH < TΔS
C) ΔH = TΔS
D) None of the above
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: ΔG = ΔH – TΔS. For ΔG to be negative when both ΔH and ΔS are positive, TΔS must be greater than ΔH.

Q: Which of the following is acceptable as the organic product of the reaction of ethanoic acid and ethanol with an acid catalyst?
A) Butanol
B) ethyl methyl ketone
C) methyl ethanoate
D) ethyl ethanoate
ANSWER: D
EXPLANATION: Esterification of ethanoic acid + ethanol produces ethyl ethanoate + water.
