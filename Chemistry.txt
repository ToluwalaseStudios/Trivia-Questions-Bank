CHEMISTRY – 10 QUESTIONS WITH DETAILED EXPLANATIONS

Q1: At constant volume, the heat transferred to or from a system is equal to the change in ______.
A) Internal Energy
B) Enthalpy
C) Entropy
D) Gibbs free energy
ANSWER: A
EXPLANATION: From the first law of thermodynamics, ΔU = q + w. At constant volume, no expansion work is done (w = 0), therefore q_v = ΔU. Enthalpy change equals heat at constant pressure; entropy and Gibbs free energy are state functions related to other conditions.

Q2: The reaction between hydrogen and iodine may be represented by the equation H₂(g) + I₂(g) ⇌ 2HI(g) and is exothermic. Therefore
A) an increase in temperature favours the forward reaction
B) an increase in pressure favours the backward reaction
C) both pressure and temperature must be increased to favour the forward reaction
D) a decrease in temperature will favour the forward reaction
E) a decrease in pressure and an increase in temperature will favour the forward reaction
ANSWER: D
EXPLANATION: By Le Chatelier’s principle, an exothermic reaction is favoured by a decrease in temperature (heat is treated as a product). The number of gas molecules is the same on both sides (2 = 2), so pressure has no effect on the position of equilibrium. Only option D correctly identifies the temperature effect.

Q3: The alloy duralumin is used for the building of aeroplanes because it
A) absorbs heat readily from the sun thereby preventing the inside of the plane from getting too cold at high altitudes
B) reflects sunlight readily, thereby keeping the inside of the plane cool
C) is extremely durable and possesses reflective ability
D) is easily the lightest building material
E) possesses both strength and lightness
ANSWER: E
EXPLANATION: Duralumin (an aluminium-copper alloy) combines high tensile strength with low density. This strength-to-weight ratio is the principal reason it is used in aircraft construction. The other options either misstate physical properties or are secondary considerations.

Q4: Calculate the mass, in kilograms, of magnesium produced by the electrolysis of magnesium(II) chloride in a cell operating for 24 hours at 500 amperes.
[Faraday = 96 500 C mol⁻¹, Mg = 24]
A) 2.7
B) 5.4
C) 10.8
D) 21.7
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: Quantity of electricity Q = I × t = 500 A × 24 h × 3600 s h⁻¹ = 43 200 000 C.
Moles of electrons = Q / F = 43 200 000 / 96 500 ≈ 447.67 mol.
The half-equation is Mg²⁺ + 2e⁻ → Mg, so moles of Mg = 447.67 / 2 ≈ 223.835 mol.
Mass = 223.835 × 24 g mol⁻¹ ≈ 5372 g = 5.372 kg ≈ 5.4 kg.

Q5: Two elements have the same mass number but different atomic numbers; they are ____
A) Isobars
B) Isotones
C) Allotropes
D) Isotopes
ANSWER: A
EXPLANATION: Isobars are atoms of different elements (different atomic numbers) that have the same mass number. Isotopes have the same atomic number but different mass numbers; isotones have the same number of neutrons; allotropes are different forms of the same element.

Q6: In the electrolysis of molten CuSO₄, what is produced at the cathode?
A) Copper
B) Hydrogen
C) Oxygen
D) Sulphur
ANSWER: A
EXPLANATION: In molten copper(II) sulphate the only cations present are Cu²⁺. These ions migrate to the cathode and are reduced: Cu²⁺ + 2e⁻ → Cu. Hydrogen would appear only if water were present (aqueous solution); oxygen is liberated at the anode; sulphur is not produced.

Q7: Which of the following has the lowest pH at standard conditions?
A) HI
B) HBr
C) HF
D) CH₃COOH
ANSWER: A
EXPLANATION: Acid strength of the hydrogen halides increases down the group (HI > HBr > HCl > HF) because of decreasing H–X bond strength. HI is therefore the strongest acid among the options and has the lowest pH. Acetic acid is a weak organic acid.

Q8: A gas X diffuses twice as fast as gas Y with relative molecular mass of 112. Calculate the relative molecular mass of gas X.
A) 28
B) 14
C) 56
D) 120
ANSWER: A
EXPLANATION: Graham’s law: rate ∝ 1/√M.  
(rate X / rate Y) = √(M_Y / M_X)  
2 = √(112 / M_X)  
4 = 112 / M_X  
M_X = 112 / 4 = 28.

Q9: How many sub-orbitals are found in a p-orbital?
A) 2
B) 4
C) 3
D) 5
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: A p sub-shell consists of three degenerate orbitals (p_x, p_y, p_z), each of which can hold two electrons. The question asks for the number of orbitals (sub-orbitals) within the p sub-shell, which is three.

Q10: If 5 drops of 2 mol dm⁻³ HCl is added to 10 cm³ of a solution of pH 5, the resulting solution will be
A) Greater than 5
B) Less than 5
C) Equal to 5
D) Will not change
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: Adding a strong acid (HCl) increases the concentration of H⁺ ions. The original solution has [H⁺] = 10⁻⁵ mol dm⁻³. Even a few drops of 2 mol dm⁻³ acid raise [H⁺] substantially, lowering the pH below 5.
