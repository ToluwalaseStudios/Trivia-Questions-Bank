Q: Which stage of meiosis is responsible for the separation of homologous chromosomes?
A) Anaphase I
B) Anaphase II
C) Metaphase I
D) Telophase II
ANSWER: A
EXPLANATION: Homologous chromosome pairs are pulled apart to opposite poles during Anaphase I; sister chromatids separate later, in Anaphase II.

Q: In an ecosystem, the loss of energy at each trophic level is mainly due to:
A) Photosynthesis
B) Respiration and heat loss
C) Decomposition only
D) Excretion only
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: Most energy transferred between trophic levels is lost as heat through cellular respiration and metabolic activity, which is why only about 10% passes to the next level.

Q: Which of the following is a correct pairing of a plant group with a characteristic feature?
A) Bryophytes — possess true vascular tissue
B) Pteridophytes — reproduce by seeds
C) Bryophytes — lack true roots, stems, and leaves
D) Angiosperms — lack flowers
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: Bryophytes (e.g., mosses) are non-vascular plants with rhizoids instead of true roots and lack true stems/leaves; pteridophytes reproduce by spores, not seeds.

Q: During DNA replication, which enzyme is responsible for joining Okazaki fragments on the lagging strand?
A) DNA polymerase
B) Helicase
C) DNA ligase
D) Primase
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: DNA ligase seals the nicks between adjacent Okazaki fragments, joining them into a continuous lagging strand.

Q: The mechanism by which water moves up a tall tree against gravity is best explained by:
A) Root pressure alone
B) Capillarity alone
C) Cohesion-tension theory
D) Osmosis in the xylem
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: Transpiration creates tension that pulls water upward, and cohesion between water molecules (hydrogen bonding) maintains a continuous unbroken column in the xylem — the cohesion-tension theory.

Q: Which hormone is primarily responsible for stimulating the reabsorption of water in the kidney's collecting duct?
A) Aldosterone
B) Antidiuretic hormone (ADH)
C) Insulin
D) Thyroxine
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: ADH increases the permeability of the collecting duct to water, promoting its reabsorption back into the blood and concentrating the urine.

Q: In a mangrove ecosystem, pneumatophores serve mainly to:
A) Anchor the plant more firmly in mud
B) Enable gaseous exchange in waterlogged, oxygen-poor soil
C) Store excess water
D) Facilitate pollination
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: Pneumatophores are specialized root extensions that grow upward above the waterlogged, anoxic mud, allowing oxygen uptake for root respiration.

Q: A cross between a heterozygous tall pea plant (Tt) and a homozygous short plant (tt) would be expected to produce offspring in what phenotypic ratio?
A) 3:1 tall to short
B) 1:1 tall to short
C) All tall
D) All short
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: This is a test cross (Tt × tt). Gametes combine to give Tt and tt offspring in equal proportion, giving a 1:1 tall to short ratio.

Q: Which of the following best distinguishes bacteria from viruses?
A) Viruses have a cell wall; bacteria do not
B) Bacteria are cellular and can reproduce independently; viruses are acellular and require a host cell
C) Bacteria are smaller than viruses
D) Viruses contain ribosomes; bacteria do not
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: Bacteria are living, single-celled organisms capable of independent reproduction, whereas viruses are non-cellular particles that can only replicate inside a host cell's machinery.

Q: Which structure in a woody dicot stem is primarily responsible for secondary growth in girth?
A) Epidermis
B) Vascular cambium
C) Pith
D) Cortex
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: The vascular cambium is a lateral meristem that divides to produce secondary xylem and phloem, increasing the stem's girth over time.
