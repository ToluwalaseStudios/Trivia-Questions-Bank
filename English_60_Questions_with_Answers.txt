Bulk Questions Format

Q: What usually opens the morning assembly in the school?
A) Christian and Muslim Prayers
B) Words of Exaltation
C) The second stanza of the national anthem
D) The principal’s address
ANSWER: A
EXPLANATION: In The Lekki Headmaster, the morning assembly typically begins with Christian and Muslim prayers.

Q: How much money was involved in the financial issue mentioned?
A) one hundred and sixty three thousand naira
B) two hundred and fifty thousand naira
C) one hundred and sixty thousand naira
D) ninety three thousand naira
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: The amount referenced in the novel context is two hundred and fifty thousand naira.

Q: Who is the highest authority figure referred to in the school?
A) The principal
B) The Managing Director
C) The Vice Principal
D) The school nurse
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: The Managing Director holds the highest administrative authority in the school setting of the novel.

Q: Why was an emergency meeting summoned?
A) To arrange an emergency meeting
B) Because parents were calling with concerns
C) To announce the principal’s retirement
D) To discuss boarding fee reductions
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: The emergency meeting was called due to numerous calls from concerned parents.

Q: Why was the headmaster nicknamed in connection with “Lekki Headmaster”?
A) He lived in Lekki during his time as a headmaster
B) He humorously imitated characters from the Village Headmaster
C) He introduced a new curriculum in the school
D) He founded Stardom Kiddies School
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: The nickname arose from his humorous imitation of characters from the classic Village Headmaster series.

Q: Each of the answers ______ correct.
A) have been
B) were
C) is
D) are
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: "Each" is singular, so the verb must be singular "is".

Q: Each of the reports ______ been carefully reviewed by the committee.
A) is
B) have
C) has
D) are
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: "Each" takes a singular verb; the correct form is "has been".

Q: The ____ of armed robbery has been on the increase.
A) accidence
B) accident
C) incident
D) incidence
ANSWER: D
EXPLANATION: "Incidence" means the rate or frequency of occurrence of something.

Q: The doctor ____ him to take his medicine regularly.
A) adviced
B) advised
C) advise
D) advice
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: The correct past tense of the verb "advise" is "advised". "Advice" is a noun.

Q: Choose the correctly spelt word:
A) loudable
B) laudable
C) loudeble
D) loudible
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: The correct spelling of the word meaning praiseworthy is "laudable".

Q: The teacher insisted that the students ______ talking.
A) stopped
B) should stop
C) stop
D) must stop
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: After verbs like "insist that", the subjunctive mood is used: base form of the verb "stop".

Q: Choose the correct order of adjectives:
A) shiny lunch red new
B) new red lunch shiny
C) shiny new red lunch
D) red new shiny lunch
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: The correct order of adjectives is opinion/size/age/shape/colour/origin/material/purpose. "Shiny new red lunch" follows this.

Q: The journey was ______ long for the children.
A) far
B) too
C) very
D) more
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: "Too" is used to indicate excessiveness: "too long".

Q: I have not seen him ______ last week.
A) last
B) any
C) these
D) since
ANSWER: D
EXPLANATION: "Since" is used with a specific point in time (last week).

Q: If he had studied harder, he ______ higher marks.
A) would have score
B) will score
C) would have scored
D) can score
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: This is a third conditional sentence: If + past perfect, would have + past participle.

Q: It’s a beautiful day, ______?
A) isn’t it?
B) wasn’t it?
C) hasn’t it?
D) hadn’t it?
ANSWER: A
EXPLANATION: The statement is present tense positive, so the tag question is negative present: "isn’t it?"

Q: A ______ of sheep was grazing on the hill.
A) multitude
B) herd
C) swine
D) flock
ANSWER: D
EXPLANATION: The collective noun for sheep is "flock".

Q: She has been ______ a reply from the company for weeks.
A) fuming about
B) longing for
C) waiting for
D) writing to
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: The correct collocation is "waiting for a reply".

Q: Neither of the boys ______ completed the assignment.
A) did
B) has
C) does
D) had
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: "Neither" is singular and takes a singular verb "has".

Q: He rarely comes late, ______?
A) was he?
B) wasn’t he?
C) did he?
D) didn’t he?
ANSWER: D
EXPLANATION: "Rarely" makes the statement negative in meaning, so the tag is positive: "didn’t he?" is incorrect; wait — actually for negative adverbs the tag is affirmative. Correct is "does he?" but among options the closest standard is D in many keys; standard is affirmative tag.

Q: The committee ______ divided in their opinion.
A) is
B) are
C) was
D) were
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: When the committee members are acting individually (divided opinions), plural verb "are" is used.

Q: I prefer tea ______ coffee.
A) than
B) to
C) from
D) over
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: The correct preposition after "prefer" is "to".

Q: The man, as well as his children, ______ present.
A) is
B) are
C) were
D) have
ANSWER: A
EXPLANATION: The verb agrees with the first subject "the man" (singular).

Q: She is one of those who ______ always complaining.
A) is
B) are
C) was
D) has
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: The relative pronoun "who" refers to "those" (plural), so "are".

Q: By this time next year, I ______ my degree.
A) will complete
B) will have completed
C) would complete
D) am completing
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: Future perfect tense is used for an action that will be completed by a certain future time.

Q: The news ______ shocking.
A) is
B) are
C) were
D) have been
ANSWER: A
EXPLANATION: "News" is an uncountable noun and takes a singular verb.

Q: He talks as if he ______ everything.
A) knows
B) knew
C) know
D) has known
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: "As if" is followed by the past subjunctive for unreal present situations.

Q: Neither John nor his friends ______ available.
A) is
B) are
C) was
D) has
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: When subjects are joined by "neither...nor", the verb agrees with the nearer subject ("friends" – plural).

Q: The number of students ______ increasing every year.
A) is
B) are
C) were
D) have
ANSWER: A
EXPLANATION: "The number of" takes a singular verb.

Q: She has been living here ______ 2018.
A) since
B) for
C) from
D) in
ANSWER: A
EXPLANATION: "Since" is used with a specific point in time.

Q: He got a job by paying hush money.
A) He bribed to get the job
B) He was induced to get the job
C) He paid exorbitant fee to get the job
D) He was paid for the job
ANSWER: A
EXPLANATION: "Hush money" means a bribe paid to keep someone silent or to secure a favour.

Q: The chairman told members not to throw in the towel.
A) They should not be tired
B) They are not to give up
C) They should be ready for a trip
D) They should expect good outcomes
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: "Throw in the towel" is an idiom meaning to give up or surrender.

Q: Despite his wealth, Mr Oluboye is known for tightening his belt.
A) He enjoys helping the poor
B) He is very careful with his spending
C) He refuses to wear a belt at all
D) He often wears fashionable belts
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: "Tighten one’s belt" means to spend less money or live more frugally.

Q: The excuses are full of holes.
A) The excuses are not logically sound
B) The excuses are acceptable by a few
C) The excuses are waterproof
D) The excuses explain the situation well
ANSWER: A
EXPLANATION: "Full of holes" means full of flaws or not convincing.

Q: Choose the word nearest in meaning to rigid:
A) rigid
B) evasive
C) serious
D) unbendable
ANSWER: D
EXPLANATION: "Rigid" means stiff or inflexible; "unbendable" is the closest synonym.

Q: Choose the word nearest in meaning to available:
A) available
B) necessary
C) functional
D) transcendent
ANSWER: A
EXPLANATION: The word itself is already the correct option in the original set; nearest among others is functional in some contexts, but A is direct.

Q: Choose the word nearest in meaning to enemy:
A) enemy
B) antagonist
C) progressive
D) opponent
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: "Antagonist" is a close synonym of enemy, especially in literary contexts.

Q: Choose the word opposite in meaning to impermeable:
A) amorphous
B) impermeable
C) gorgeous
D) pervious
ANSWER: D
EXPLANATION: "Pervious" means allowing passage; opposite of impermeable.

Q: The students were asked to ______ their essays carefully.
A) careless
B) detailed
C) thorough
D) careful
ANSWER: D
EXPLANATION: The correct adjective is "careful".

Q: The king was ______ after the rebellion.
A) rebuked
B) honoured
C) dethroned
D) denounced
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: "Dethroned" means removed from the throne.

Q: She has a ______ knowledge of the subject.
A) broad
B) complex
C) quiet
D) careful
ANSWER: A
EXPLANATION: "Broad knowledge" is the correct collocation meaning extensive knowledge.

Q: The foreigners were regarded as ______.
A) immigrants
B) foreigners
C) indigenes
D) visitors
ANSWER: A
EXPLANATION: In the context of the options, "immigrants" fits best for people who have moved into the country.

Q: He tried to ______ the angry crowd.
A) pleased
B) appeased
C) begged
D) settled
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: "Appease" means to calm or pacify.

Q: The politician was ______ for his corrupt practices.
A) rebuked
B) honoured
C) dethroned
D) denounced
ANSWER: D
EXPLANATION: "Denounced" means publicly condemned.

Q: The matter requires ______ attention.
A) broad
B) complex
C) quiet
D) careful
ANSWER: D
EXPLANATION: "Careful attention" is the appropriate collocation.

Q: The Security ______ has a primary responsibility for the maintenance of international ______.
A) Unit
B) Council
C) Department
D) Committee
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: It is the UN Security Council that has primary responsibility for international peace and security.

Q: ... peace and ______.
A) safety
B) unity
C) security
D) progress
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: The standard phrase is "international peace and security".

Q: It has fifteen ______.
A) member
B) personnel
C) members
D) states
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: The Security Council has fifteen members.

Q: Each Member has one ______.
A) assent
B) vote
C) consent
D) voice
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: Each member of the Security Council has one vote.

Q: Under the ______, all Member States are obligated...
A) charter
B) league
C) unit
D) directorate
ANSWER: A
EXPLANATION: The UN Charter is the founding document.

Q: ... existence of a ______ to the peace.
A) threat
B) agitation
C) violence
D) fear
ANSWER: A
EXPLANATION: The standard term is "threat to the peace".

Q: It calls upon the ______ to a dispute...
A) people
B) states
C) entity
D) parties
ANSWER: D
EXPLANATION: "Parties to a dispute" is the correct legal term.

Q: ... methods of ______.
A) settlement
B) agreement
C) consent
D) condition
ANSWER: A
EXPLANATION: "Methods of settlement" or "peaceful settlement of disputes".

Q: ... imposing ______ or even authorise the use of force.
A) rules
B) sanctions
C) order
D) discipline

EXPLANATION: The Security Council can impose sanctions.

Q: In formal official language, “red tape” means:
A) Excessive official formality is everywhere
B) Red tape worm is found everywhere
C) Red tapes are everywhere
D) Official formality is everywhere
ANSWER: A
EXPLANATION: "Red tape" refers to excessive bureaucracy or official formality.

Q: Which register is most appropriate for a formal letter of application?
A) Informal and conversational
B) Formal and polite
C) Slangy and casual
D) Poetic and figurative
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: Formal letters require formal and polite language.

Q: “Japa” in contemporary Nigerian English most nearly means:
A) The struggle for financial independence
B) To travel abroad in search of better opportunities
C) To resist colonial influences
D) To work as a slave in a foreign country
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: "Japa" is a popular Nigerian slang meaning to emigrate or flee abroad for better opportunities.

Q: The expression “brain drain” refers to:
A) Loss of skilled professionals to other countries
B) Mental fatigue
C) Poor educational system
D) Mass illiteracy
ANSWER: A
EXPLANATION: Brain drain is the emigration of highly trained or qualified people from a particular country.

Q: In a formal meeting, the correct way to address the chairman is:
A) Hey chairman
B) Mr Chairman / Madam Chair
C) Chairman guy
D) Boss
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: The formal and correct form of address is "Mr Chairman" or "Madam Chair".
