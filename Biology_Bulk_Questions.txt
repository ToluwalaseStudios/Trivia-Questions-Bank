Q: Which organelle is primarily responsible for the production of ATP during aerobic respiration?
A) Ribosome
B) Lysosome
C) Mitochondrion
D) Golgi apparatus
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: The mitochondrion is the site of aerobic respiration, where the Krebs cycle and electron transport chain generate most of the cell's ATP.

Q: A plant cell placed in a concentrated sugar solution loses water and its cell membrane pulls away from the cell wall. This process is known as:
A) Diffusion
B) Turgidity
C) Plasmolysis
D) Active transport
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: Plasmolysis occurs when a plant cell in a hypertonic solution loses water by osmosis, causing the cell membrane to shrink away from the rigid cell wall.

Q: Which structure controls the movement of substances into and out of a cell?
A) Cell wall
B) Plasma membrane
C) Cytoplasm
D) Nucleus
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: The plasma membrane is selectively permeable and regulates which substances enter or leave the cell.

Q: The main significance of mitosis in multicellular organisms is:
A) Production of gametes
B) Reduction of chromosome number
C) Growth and replacement of damaged cells
D) Production of genetically different cells
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: Mitosis produces genetically identical daughter cells, which enables growth and the replacement of worn-out or damaged tissue.

Q: If a human body cell contains 46 chromosomes, how many chromosomes would normally be present in each daughter cell after mitosis?
A) 23
B) 46
C) 69
D) 92
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: Mitosis produces daughter cells that are genetically identical to the parent cell, so the chromosome number (46) stays the same.

Q: Which taxonomic group contains organisms that have a true nucleus and membrane-bound organelles?
A) Prokaryotae
B) Eukaryotae
C) Viruses
D) Bacteria only
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: Eukaryotae comprises organisms whose cells possess a true, membrane-bound nucleus and other membrane-bound organelles.

Q: Which sequence correctly represents the major taxonomic hierarchy from the broadest to the most specific?
A) Kingdom → Class → Phylum → Order → Family → Genus → Species
B) Kingdom → Phylum → Class → Order → Family → Genus → Species
C) Species → Genus → Family → Order → Class → Phylum → Kingdom
D) Kingdom → Order → Class → Family → Phylum → Genus → Species
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: The standard biological classification hierarchy, from broadest to most specific, is Kingdom, Phylum, Class, Order, Family, Genus, Species.

Q: Which characteristic is most useful in distinguishing mammals from other vertebrates?
A) Presence of a backbone
B) Presence of lungs
C) Presence of hair and mammary glands
D) Ability to reproduce sexually
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: Hair and mammary glands (for producing milk) are unique defining features of mammals not shared by other vertebrate classes.

Q: An organism possesses chlorophyll, has cellulose cell walls and stores carbohydrates mainly as starch. It is most likely a:
A) Fungus
B) Plant
C) Bacterium
D) Protozoan
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: Chlorophyll for photosynthesis, cellulose cell walls, and starch as a storage carbohydrate are characteristic features of plants.

Q: Which of the following organisms belongs to the kingdom Fungi?
A) Amoeba
B) Mushroom
C) Euglena
D) Spirogyra
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: A mushroom is a fungus; Amoeba and Euglena are protists/protozoans, and Spirogyra is an alga.
