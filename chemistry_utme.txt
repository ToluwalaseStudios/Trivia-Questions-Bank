Q: What is the IUPAC name of CH₃-CH(OH)-CH₃?
A) Propan-1-ol
B) Propan-2-ol
C) Propanal
D) Propanone
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: The -OH group is on the middle (second) carbon of the three-carbon chain, giving propan-2-ol.

Q: According to Le Chatelier's Principle, increasing the pressure on the equilibrium system N₂(g) + 3H₂(g) ⇌ 2NH₃(g) will:
A) Shift equilibrium to the left
B) Shift equilibrium to the right
C) Have no effect
D) Stop the reaction
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: There are 4 moles of gas on the left and 2 moles on the right. Increasing pressure shifts equilibrium toward the side with fewer gas moles, i.e., to the right (more NH₃).

Q: What volume of oxygen (at STP) is required to completely burn 5.6 dm³ of methane gas according to CH₄ + 2O₂ → CO₂ + 2H₂O?
A) 2.8 dm³
B) 5.6 dm³
C) 11.2 dm³
D) 22.4 dm³
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: Mole ratio of CH₄:O₂ is 1:2. So volume of O₂ required = 2 × 5.6 = 11.2 dm³.

Q: Which of the following is the correct order of increasing oxidizing power of the halogens?
A) F₂ < Cl₂ < Br₂ < I₂
B) I₂ < Br₂ < Cl₂ < F₂
C) Cl₂ < F₂ < Br₂ < I₂
D) Br₂ < I₂ < F₂ < Cl₂
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: Oxidizing power of halogens decreases down Group 7, so it increases up the group: I₂ is the weakest oxidizer and F₂ the strongest.

Q: In the Solvay process, which raw material provides the ammonia used to saturate the brine?
A) Fresh ammonia only, consumed entirely
B) Ammonia is recycled from ammonium chloride decomposition
C) Ammonia comes from nitric acid
D) Ammonia is not used in the process
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: In the Solvay process, ammonia is largely recovered and recycled when ammonium chloride is treated with lime (calcium hydroxide), making the process economical.

Q: A first-order reaction has a rate constant of 0.02 s⁻¹. What is its half-life?
A) 17.3 s
B) 25 s
C) 34.7 s
D) 50 s
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: For a first-order reaction, t½ = 0.693/k = 0.693/0.02 ≈ 34.7 s.

Q: Which functional group test distinguishes an aldehyde from a ketone?
A) Litmus test
B) Fehling's solution test
C) Sodium metal test
D) Bromine water test
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: Aldehydes reduce Fehling's solution (giving a brick-red precipitate of Cu₂O) because they are easily oxidized; ketones generally do not react.

Q: What is the pH of a 0.001 mol/dm³ solution of HCl (assume complete dissociation)?
A) 1
B) 2
C) 3
D) 11
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: [H+] = 0.001 = 10⁻³ mol/dm³, so pH = -log(10⁻³) = 3.

Q: Which statement about electronegativity across Period 3 (Na to Cl) is correct?
A) It decreases across the period
B) It increases across the period
C) It remains constant
D) It increases then decreases
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: Across a period, nuclear charge increases while shielding stays roughly constant, pulling bonding electrons more strongly, so electronegativity increases from Na to Cl.

Q: Which of the following best explains why Mg fails to follow the general trend of reactivity expected for Group 2 in its reaction with cold water?
A) Mg has a smaller atomic radius than Ca, so it reacts faster
B) Mg forms a protective oxide/hydroxide layer that slows reaction with cold water
C) Mg does not react with water under any conditions
D) Mg has a lower melting point than Ca
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: Mg reacts very slowly with cold water because a thin, poorly soluble layer of magnesium hydroxide forms on its surface, protecting it from further reaction (it does react readily with steam, however).
