Q1.
Question: What is the derivative of $f(x) = x^3 - 4x + 7$ with respect to $x$?
A. $3x^2 - 4$
B. $3x^2 - 4x$
C. $x^2 - 4$
D. $3x^3 - 4$
Correct Answer: A
Explanation: Applying the power rule to $x^3 - 4x + 7$ yields $3x^{3-1} - 4(1) + 0 = 3x^2 - 4$.

Q2.
Question: What is the sum of the interior angles of a convex polygon with $n$ sides?
A. $(n-2) \times 180^\circ$
B. $(n+2) \times 180^\circ$
C. $n \times 180^\circ$
D. $(2n-4) \times 90^\circ$
Correct Answer: A
Explanation: The formula for the sum of interior angles in an $n$-gon is $(n-2) \times 180^\circ$.

Q3.
Question: What is the value of $\log_{10}(1000)$?
A. 2
B. 3
C. 4
D. 10
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Since $10^3 = 1000$, $\log_{10}(1000) = 3$.

Q4.
Question: What is the solution to the equation $2x + 5 = 15$?
A. $x = 10$
B. $x = 5$
C. $x = 7.5$
D. $x = 2.5$
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Subtract 5 from both sides ($2x = 10$), then divide by 2 ($x = 5$).

Q5.
Question: What is the value of $\sin(\frac{\pi}{6})$?
A. $\frac{1}{2}$
B. $\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$
C. $\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$
D. 1
Correct Answer: A
Explanation: $\sin(30^\circ) = \sin(\frac{\pi}{6}) = \frac{1}{2}$.
