A) follicle
B) legume
C) capsule
D) schizocarp
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: A capsule is specifically defined as a dry, multi-carpellate fruit that dehisces (splits open) to release several seeds — distinguishing it from a follicle or legume (typically single carpel) or a schizocarp (which splits into distinct one-seeded segments rather than releasing seeds from within a single unified fruit).

Q: The male reproductive organ of a flower is the
A) stamen
B) petal
C) sepal
D) carpel
ANSWER: A
EXPLANATION: The stamen (comprising the anther and filament) is specifically the male reproductive organ of a flower, producing pollen; the carpel, by contrast, is the female reproductive organ.

Q: An example of an organ of perennation in plants is
A) rhizome
B) seed
C) petal of a flower
D) calyx of flower
ANSWER: A
EXPLANATION: An organ of perennation is a structure that allows a plant to survive unfavourable conditions and persist from one growing season to the next — a rhizome (an underground stem storing food and capable of regrowth) is a classic example, unlike petals or calyx, which are temporary flower parts, or seeds, which represent a new generation rather than the survival/persistence of the same individual plant.

Q: (I) Growth is mainly apical. (II) Growth is specific with definite shape. (III) Growth is throughout life. Which of the above correctly describes the growth pattern in plants?
A) I, II and III only
B) II and III only
C) I and II only
D) I and III only
ANSWER: D
EXPLANATION: Plant growth is indeed mainly apical (concentrated at growing tips/meristems) and continues throughout the plant's entire life (unlike animals, which typically stop growing after reaching maturity) — matching statements I and III. However, plants do not grow into one single, fixed, "definite" shape the way many animals do; their form remains comparatively indeterminate and variable throughout life, making statement II inaccurate.
