Q: The sum of the coefficients of all species (reactants and products) in the balanced equation for the reaction between aqueous lead(II) nitrate and sodium phosphate is
A) 4
B) 6
C) 10
D) 12
ANSWER: D
EXPLANATION: 3Pb(NO₃)₂ + 2Na₃PO₄ → Pb₃(PO₄)₂ + 6NaNO₃. Coefficients: 3 + 2 + 1 + 6 = 12.

Q: The volume occupied by a certain mass of hydrogen gas collected over water at 28 °C and 769 mmHg was 35 cm³. What would be the volume of the dry gas at S.T.P. if the saturated vapour pressure of water at 28 °C is 13.5 mmHg?
A) 35.00 cm³
B) 32.42 cm³
C) 31.60 cm³
D) 17.30 cm³
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: Pressure of dry gas = 769 − 13.5 = 755.5 mmHg. Using P₁V₁/T₁ = P₂V₂/T₂. V₂ = (755.5 × 35 × 273) / (760 × 301) ≈ 31.6 cm³.

Q: 50 cm³ of 0.1 mol dm⁻³ AgNO₃ and 50 cm³ of 0.1 mol dm⁻³ CaCl₂ solutions are mixed. Assuming AgCl is completely insoluble, the mass of AgCl formed is
A) 0.98 g
B) 1.43 g
C) 0.72 g
D) 0.36 g
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: 2AgNO₃ + CaCl₂ → 2AgCl + Ca(NO₃)₂. Moles of Ag⁺ = 0.005; moles of Cl⁻ = 0.01. Limiting reagent is Ag⁺ → 0.005 mol AgCl. Mass = 0.005 × 143.5 ≈ 0.72 g.

Q: An ion Xⁿ⁺ has 14 electrons. How many unpaired electrons are there in the neutral atom of X?
A) 4
B) 3
C) 2
D) 1
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: If Xⁿ⁺ has 14 electrons, the neutral atom has 14 + n electrons. For common ions (e.g., Si²⁺ or similar) the neutral atom configuration ends with two unpaired electrons in the p-orbital.

Q: Sodium chloride can be separated from rock salt by first adding water to dissolve the sodium chloride. The further separation stages are
A) filtration followed by decantation

C) distillation followed by decantation
D) filtration followed by evaporation
ANSWER: D
EXPLANATION: Insoluble impurities are removed by filtration; the filtrate is then evaporated to recover pure NaCl crystals.

Q: Which of the following statements about molecular shapes is correct?

Q: A hydrated salt of formula FeSO₄·xH₂O contains 45.3 % of water of crystallisation. The value of x is [Fe = 56, S = 32, O = 16, H = 1]
A) 7
B) 6
C) 5
D) 4
ANSWER: A
EXPLANATION: Molar mass of FeSO₄ = 152. Mass of water = 18x. (18x)/(152 + 18x) = 0.453. Solving gives 18x = 0.453(152 + 18x) → x ≈ 7.

Q: For the reaction 2X(g) + Y(g) → Z(g), the rate of formation of Z doubles when [Y] is doubled and quadruples when [X] is doubled. The rate expression is
A) R = k[X][Y]
B) R = k[X]²[Y]
C) R = k[X][Y]²
D) R = k[X]²[Y]²
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: Rate ∝ [X]² (quadruples when [X] doubles) and Rate ∝ [Y]¹ (doubles when [Y] doubles). Therefore R = k[X]²[Y].

Q: A chemical reaction proceeds with an increase in both enthalpy and entropy. The reaction will be spontaneous if
A) ΔH > TΔS
B) ΔH < TΔS
C) ΔH = TΔS
D) ΔH is negative
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: ΔG = ΔH − TΔS. For spontaneity ΔG < 0, which requires TΔS > ΔH when both ΔH and ΔS are positive.
