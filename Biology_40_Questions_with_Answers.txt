Bulk Questions Format

Q: The process that brings about the cooling effect on the body of mammals is
A) sweat excretion
B) digestion
C) urine formation
D) respiration
ANSWER: A
EXPLANATION: Evaporation of sweat from the skin surface removes latent heat, thereby cooling the body.

Q: Which of the following controls protein and enzyme syntheses in the cytoplasm of the cell?
A) DNA
B) Nucleus
C) Chromosome
D) Mitochondria
ANSWER: A
EXPLANATION: DNA contains the genetic code that is transcribed into mRNA, which directs protein synthesis in the cytoplasm.

Q: The type of germination in which the cotyledons are brought above the soil is called
A) epicotyl
B) epigeal
C) hypocotyl
D) hypogeal
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: In epigeal germination the hypocotyl elongates and pulls the cotyledons above the ground.

Q: A cross between a homozygous tall plant and a homozygous short plant produces
A) Homozygous tall
B) Homozygous short
C) Heterozygous short
D) Heterozygous tall
ANSWER: D
EXPLANATION: Tall is usually dominant; the F1 generation is heterozygous tall (Tt).

Q: Which of the following is an example of continuous variation?
A) tongue rolling
B) height
C) colour
D) fingerprints
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: Height shows a continuous range of values and is influenced by many genes and the environment.

Q: The relationship in which energy flows from one organism to another is called
A) food web
B) pyramid of numbers
C) pyramid of energy
D) food chain
ANSWER: D
EXPLANATION: A food chain shows the unidirectional flow of energy from one organism to another.

Q: The process by which water vapour is lost from the leaves of plants is
A) diffusion
B) photosynthesis
C) osmosis
D) transpiration
ANSWER: D
EXPLANATION: Transpiration is the loss of water vapour from the aerial parts of plants, mainly through stomata.

Q: Which of the following is a characteristic of a climax community?
A) resistant crop varieties
B) susceptible to diseases
C) of low quality
D) low yielding varieties
ANSWER: A
EXPLANATION: A climax community is stable and consists of species well adapted (resistant) to the prevailing conditions.

Q: The conversion of ammonium compounds to nitrates is known as
A) Combustion
B) Nitrification
C) Evaporation
D) Transpiration
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: Nitrification is the biological oxidation of ammonia to nitrite and then to nitrate by nitrifying bacteria.

Q: Organisms that feed on dead organic matter are called
A) producers
B) consumers
C) decomposers
D) parasites
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: Decomposers (bacteria and fungi) break down dead organic matter and recycle nutrients.

Q: Flowers that are pollinated by wind usually have
A) large anthers
B) absence of nectar
C) scented flowers
D) scentless flowers
ANSWER: D
EXPLANATION: Wind-pollinated flowers are typically small, inconspicuous, scentless and produce abundant light pollen.

Q: The supporting tissues in plants include
A) sclerenchyma and collenchyma
B) collenchyma and xylem
C) Sclerenchyma and parenchyma
D) parenchyma and collenchyma
ANSWER: A
EXPLANATION: Collenchyma provides flexible support in growing parts while sclerenchyma provides rigid support.

Q: Plants that bear cones belong to the group
A) fern
B) angiosperm
C) cones
D) gymnosperm
ANSWER: D
EXPLANATION: Gymnosperms (e.g. pines, cycads) produce naked seeds usually borne in cones.

Q: The fluid found in the vacuole of plant cells is
A) Latex
B) Cytoplasm
C) Cell sap
D) Blood
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: The vacuole of plant cells contains cell sap, a solution of salts, sugars and other substances.

Q: The part of the flower that protects the bud is the
A) corolla
B) pedicel
C) calyx
D) receptacle
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: The calyx (sepals) encloses and protects the flower bud before it opens.

Q: The gland that secretes thyroxine is the
A) parathyroid
B) thyroid
C) pituitary
D) adrenal
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: The thyroid gland secretes the hormone thyroxine which regulates metabolic rate.

Q: The part of the central nervous system responsible for coordination of balance is the
A) cerebellum
B) brain
C) spinal cord
D) sensory neurones
ANSWER: A
EXPLANATION: The cerebellum coordinates voluntary movements and maintains posture and balance.

Q: Which of the following is true of poliomyelitis?
A) It affects both young and old
B) It is a bacterial disease
C) There is no vaccination against the disease
D) It is capable of paralysing the limbs
ANSWER: D
EXPLANATION: Poliomyelitis is a viral disease that can cause paralysis of the limbs; vaccination is available.

Q: The process of removing metabolic waste products from the body is called
A) digestion
B) excretion
C) respiration
D) egestion
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: Excretion is the removal of metabolic wastes (e.g. urea, CO₂) from the body.

Q: The respiratory organ in man is the
A) gill
B) lung
C) skin
D) trachea
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: Humans use lungs for gaseous exchange.

Q: The enzyme that digests starch is
A) pepsin
B) lipase
C) amylase
D) trypsin
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: Amylase (in saliva and pancreatic juice) hydrolyses starch to maltose.

Q: Bile is produced by the
A) pancreas
B) liver
C) gall bladder
D) stomach
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: Bile is produced by the liver and stored in the gall bladder.

Q: The end product of protein digestion is
A) glucose
B) fatty acids
C) amino acids
D) glycerol
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: Proteins are hydrolysed by proteases into amino acids.

Q: Vitamin necessary for blood clotting is
A) Vitamin A
B) Vitamin B
C) Vitamin C
D) Vitamin K
ANSWER: D
EXPLANATION: Vitamin K is essential for the synthesis of prothrombin and other clotting factors.

Q: Kwashiorkor is caused by deficiency of
A) carbohydrate
B) protein
C) fat
D) vitamin
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: Kwashiorkor is a protein-deficiency disease common in children.

Q: The male reproductive organ in flowering plants is the
A) stigma
B) ovary
C) anther
D) style
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: The anther produces pollen grains (male gametes).

Q: Fertilisation in humans takes place in the
A) ovary
B) uterus
C) vagina
D) fallopian tube
ANSWER: D
EXPLANATION: Fertilisation normally occurs in the ampulla of the fallopian tube (oviduct).

Q: The process by which a mature ovum is released is called
A) menstruation
B) ovulation
C) fertilisation
D) implantation
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: Ovulation is the release of a mature ovum from the ovary.

Q: Secondary sexual characteristics in males are controlled by
A) oestrogen
B) progesterone
C) testosterone
D) insulin
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: Testosterone, produced by the testes, is responsible for male secondary sexual characteristics.

Q: The period of pregnancy in humans is called
A) gestation
B) ovulation
C) menstruation
D) lactation
ANSWER: A
EXPLANATION: Gestation is the period from fertilisation to birth (approximately 40 weeks in humans).

Q: Insects belong to the phylum
A) Annelida
B) Arthropoda
C) Mollusca
D) Chordata
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: Insects are arthropods characterised by jointed appendages and an exoskeleton.

Q: The scientific name of man is
A) Homo erectus
B) Homo sapiens
C) Homo habilis
D) Homo neanderthalensis
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: The binomial name of modern humans is Homo sapiens.

Q: Organisms without a true nucleus are called
A) eukaryotes
B) prokaryotes
C) multicellular
D) unicellular
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: Prokaryotes (bacteria and archaea) lack a membrane-bound nucleus.

Q: The largest group in classification is
A) species
B) genus
C) family
D) kingdom
ANSWER: D
EXPLANATION: In the hierarchical system of classification, kingdom is the highest (largest) taxonomic rank.

Q: Birds are characterised by the presence of
A) scales
B) feathers
C) fur
D) slime
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: Feathers are a unique characteristic of birds (class Aves).

Q: The long neck of the giraffe is an example of
A) structural adaptation
B) behavioural adaptation
C) physiological adaptation
D) protective adaptation
ANSWER: A
EXPLANATION: The long neck is a structural (morphological) adaptation that enables the giraffe to feed on high foliage.

Q: Natural selection was proposed by
A) Lamarck
B) Darwin
C) Mendel
D) Pasteur
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: Charles Darwin proposed the theory of evolution by natural selection.

Q: The theory that acquired characters can be inherited was proposed by
A) Darwin
B) Lamarck
C) Wallace
D) Mendel
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: Jean-Baptiste Lamarck proposed the theory of inheritance of acquired characteristics.

Q: Homologous structures indicate
A) convergent evolution
B) divergent evolution
C) parallel evolution
D) adaptive radiation
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: Homologous structures (e.g. forelimbs of vertebrates) indicate divergent evolution from a common ancestor.

Q: The ability of an organism to maintain a constant internal environment is called
A) adaptation
B) homeostasis
C) metabolism
D) irritability
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: Homeostasis is the maintenance of a stable internal environment despite external changes.
