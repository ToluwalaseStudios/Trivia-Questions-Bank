Q: Choose the option that best completes the sentence: "Neither the manager nor the workers _____ satisfied with the new policy."
A) is
B) are
C) was
D) has been
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: With "neither...nor," the verb agrees with the subject nearer to it — here "the workers" (plural) — so "are" is correct.

Q: Identify the correctly punctuated sentence.
A) "Its a beautiful day," she said.
B) "It's a beautiful day," she said.
C) "Its' a beautiful day," she said.
D) "It is a beautiful day", she said
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: "It's" is the correct contraction of "it is." The comma belongs inside the closing quotation mark before "she said."

Q: Select the word nearest in meaning to "MERETRICIOUS."
A) Genuine
B) Showy but worthless
C) Deserving
D) Modest
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: "Meretricious" describes something that is superficially or garishly attractive but actually has no real value.

Q: Select the word opposite in meaning to "PARSIMONIOUS."
A) Stingy
B) Generous
C) Careful
D) Wealthy
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: "Parsimonious" means extremely unwilling to spend money; its opposite is "generous."

Q: Choose the option that best completes the sentence: "By the time the fire fighters arrived, the whole building _____ down."
A) burns
B) had burnt
C) has burnt
D) was burning
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: The past perfect tense ("had burnt") is used because the burning was completed before another past action (the fire fighters' arrival).

Q: Which of the following sentences is in the passive voice?
A) The committee approved the budget.
B) The budget was approved by the committee.
C) The committee is approving the budget.
D) The committee will approve the budget.
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: In passive voice, the subject receives the action. Here "the budget" (the object of the action) becomes the subject, with "was approved by" showing the passive construction.

Q: Identify the correct rendering of the given stress pattern for the word "PHOTOGRAPHY."
A) Stress on the first syllable
B) Stress on the second syllable
C) Stress on the third syllable
D) Stress on the last syllable
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: "Photography" is stressed as pho-TOG-ra-phy, with primary stress on the second syllable.

Q: Choose the option that correctly identifies the type of clause underlined: "The man who stole the bicycle has been arrested."
A) Noun clause
B) Adverbial clause
C) Relative (adjectival) clause
D) Independent clause
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: "Who stole the bicycle" describes/modifies the noun "the man," which is the function of a relative (adjectival) clause.

Q: Select the option nearest in meaning to the idiom "to let the cat out of the bag."
A) To adopt a pet
B) To reveal a secret unintentionally
C) To cause confusion
D) To escape danger
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: This idiom means to accidentally disclose a secret or piece of hidden information.

Q: Choose the option with the correct spelling.
A) Accomodate
B) Acommodate
C) Accommodate
D) Accomadate
