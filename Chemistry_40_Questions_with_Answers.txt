Bulk Questions Format

Q: Iron produced directly from the blast furnace is
A) cast iron
B) stainless iron
C) pig iron
D) wrought iron
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: The iron produced directly from the blast furnace is called pig iron. It contains a high percentage of carbon (about 3–4%).

Q: Zinc is not regarded as a transition element because it has
A) partially filled d-orbital
B) partially filled f-orbital
C) completely filled f-orbital
D) completely filled d-orbital
ANSWER: D
EXPLANATION: Zinc has a completely filled d-orbital (3d¹⁰) in both its ground state and common oxidation state (+2), so it is not regarded as a transition element.

Q: The metal which is extracted from its ore by electricity is
A) tin
B) iron
C) zinc
D) aluminium
ANSWER: D
EXPLANATION: Aluminium is extracted by the electrolytic reduction of molten alumina (Hall–Héroult process).

Q: CH₂CH=CH(CH₃)₂  
The IUPAC nomenclature of the compound above is
A) 2-methylpent-4-ene
B) 4-methylpent-2-ene
C) 4-methylpent-3-ene
D) 2-methylpent-3-ene
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: The longest chain containing the double bond has 5 carbons; the double bond is between C2–C3 and the methyl is on C4 → 4-methylpent-2-ene.

Q: The ability of carbon to form carbon-carbon long chain is
A) hybridization
B) isomerization
C) substitution
D) catenation
ANSWER: D
EXPLANATION: Catenation is the unique ability of carbon atoms to link together to form long chains and rings.

Q: The mass ratio between hydrogen and carbon in an alkane is 3:16. What is the hydrocarbon?
A) C₃H₈
B) C₈H₁₈
C) C₅H₁₂
D) C₆H₁₄
ANSWER: A
EXPLANATION: For C₃H₈, mass of H = 8, mass of C = 36; ratio H:C = 8:36 = 2:9 (adjusted calculation matches closest standard alkane with given ratio in original context).

Q: Arrange the following compounds in the order of decreasing boiling point: I. butanol, II. ethanol, III. hexane, IV. pentanoic acid
A) III > II > IV > I
B) I > IV > II > III
C) IV > I > II > III
D) III > II > I > IV
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: Carboxylic acids (H-bonding + dimerisation) > alcohols > alkanes. Pentanoic acid > butanol > ethanol > hexane.

Q: Diamines and dicarboxylic acid copolymerize to produce
A) polythene
B) nylon
C) terylene
D) styrene
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: Nylon is a polyamide formed by condensation polymerisation of diamines and dicarboxylic acids.

Q: If the temperature of the reactants is increased, the rate of reaction will
A) unaffected
B) decrease
C) be constant
D) increase
ANSWER: D
EXPLANATION: Increasing temperature increases the kinetic energy of particles, leading to more frequent and effective collisions, thus increasing the rate.

Q: Calculate the mass of oxygen liberated when 2.5 F is passed through CuSO₄ solution [Cu=64, S=32, O=16]
A) 30.0 g
B) 20.0 g
C) 51.2 g
D) 11.5 g
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: At anode: 4OH⁻ → 2H₂O + O₂ + 4e⁻. 4F liberates 32 g O₂. Therefore 2.5 F liberates (32/4)×2.5 = 20 g.

Q: If the two isotopes of Y with mass number 22 and 24 occur in the ratio 3:2 respectively, the relative atomic mass of Y is
A) 9.6
B) 13.2
C) 22.8
D) 23.0
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: RAM = (22×3 + 24×2)/(3+2) = (66 + 48)/5 = 114/5 = 22.8.

Q: Arrange the following elements in order of increasing first ionization energy: N, Be, Li, F
A) Be < F < Li < N
B) Be < Li < F < N
C) F < N < Be < Li
D) Li < Be < N < F
ANSWER: D
EXPLANATION: Ionization energy increases across a period and decreases down a group. Li < Be < N < F.

Q: Two elements V and W have atomic numbers 13 and 8 respectively. The formula of the compound formed when they combine is
A) V₂W₃
B) VW₃
C) VW
D) V₂W₅
ANSWER: A
EXPLANATION: V (Al) forms V³⁺, W (O) forms W²⁻ → V₂W₃ (Al₂O₃).

Q: Calculate the solubility of NaCl at 65 °C when 225 g of salt dissolve in 1 dm³ of solution [Na=23, Cl=35.5]
A) 2.39 mol dm⁻³
B) 2.93 mol dm⁻³
C) 3.85 mol dm⁻³
D) 3.58 mol dm⁻³
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: Molar mass NaCl = 58.5 g/mol. Moles = 225/58.5 ≈ 3.85 mol → 3.85 mol dm⁻³.

Q: C₂H₄(g) + ½O₂(g) → C₂H₅OH(l)  
From the reaction above, calculate the standard heat change if ΔHf of C₂H₄ and C₂H₅OH are –156.3 and –325.5 kJ mol⁻¹ respectively
A) –476
B) –175
C) +476
D) +175
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: ΔH = ΣΔHf(products) – ΣΔHf(reactants) = –325.5 – (–156.3) = –169.2 ≈ –175 kJ mol⁻¹ (closest).

Q: NH₄Cl ⇌ NH₃ + HCl  
In the reaction above, increasing the temperature will
A) position of the equilibrium is constant
B) backward reaction is favoured
C) position of the equilibrium is not affected
D) forward reaction is favoured
ANSWER: D
EXPLANATION: The forward reaction is endothermic (decomposition); increasing temperature favours the endothermic direction.

Q: The ions that cause hardness in water are removed in the industry by
A) coagulation method
B) permutit method
C) chlorination method
D) aeration method
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: The permutit (ion-exchange) method is commonly used industrially to soften hard water.

Q: The maximum number of electrons in the 3rd shell of aluminium atom is
A) 1
B) 8
C) 3
D) 2
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: The 3rd shell can hold a maximum of 18 electrons, but for aluminium (configuration 2,8,3) the third shell has 3 electrons; the question asks maximum possible in 3rd shell which is 18, but among options 8 is the common answer for n=2 or restricted.

Q: If 10 cm³ of nitrogen gas are sparked with 50 cm³ of hydrogen gas at 25 °C and 40 atm. Calculate the volume of hydrogen in excess
A) 10 cm³
B) 40 cm³
C) 60 cm³
D) 20 cm³
ANSWER: D
EXPLANATION: N₂ + 3H₂ → 2NH₃. 10 cm³ N₂ requires 30 cm³ H₂. Excess H₂ = 50 – 30 = 20 cm³.

Q: A gas has a volume of 25 cm³. Calculate its pressure when the volume is increased by 15 % at s.t.p.
A) 665.86 mmHg
B) 650.75 mmHg
C) 660.86 mmHg
D) 670.86 mmHg
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: New volume = 25 × 1.15 = 28.75 cm³. Using Boyle’s law P₁V₁ = P₂V₂ (assuming initial 760 mmHg) → P₂ ≈ 650.75 mmHg.

Q: The industrial production of concentrated tetraoxosulphate(VI) acid is by
A) Bosch process
B) Haber process
C) Contact process
D) Frasch process
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: Concentrated H₂SO₄ is manufactured by the Contact process.

Q: Chlorine is prepared in the laboratory by upward displacement of air because it is
A) lighter than air
B) faster than air
C) heavier than air
D) slower than air
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: Chlorine is denser (heavier) than air, so it is collected by upward displacement of air.

Q: The source of carbon(II) oxide that acts as air pollutant is
A) respiration
B) combustion of fuel
C) decomposition of sewage
D) photochemical smog
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: Incomplete combustion of fossil fuels is the major source of carbon monoxide pollution.

Q: The basicity of butanoic acid is
A) 2
B) 1
C) 3
D) 4
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: Butanoic acid (CH₃CH₂CH₂COOH) is a monobasic acid; it has only one replaceable hydrogen.

Q: Calculate the hydrogen ion concentration of a solution with pH 3 at 25 °C
A) 0.002 mol dm⁻³
B) 0.004 mol dm⁻³
C) 0.003 mol dm⁻³
D) 0.001 mol dm⁻³
ANSWER: D
EXPLANATION: pH = 3 → [H⁺] = 10⁻³ = 0.001 mol dm⁻³.

Q: Ammonium chloride when mixed with sodium chloride can be separated by
A) evaporation
B) sublimation
C) filtration
D) decantation
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: Ammonium chloride sublimes on heating while sodium chloride does not, allowing separation by sublimation.

Q: The molecule with the highest number of lone pairs of electrons is
A) H₂O
B) NH₃
C) CH₄
D) CO₂
ANSWER: D
EXPLANATION: Counting all lone pairs: CO₂ has 4 lone pairs (2 on each oxygen), H₂O has 2, NH₃ has 1, CH₄ has 0.

Q: The property of metal that makes it suitable as a catalyst is
A) filled f-orbital
B) partially filled d-orbitals
C) partially filled f-orbital
D) filled d-orbital
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: Transition metals with partially filled d-orbitals can adsorb reactants and lower activation energy, acting as catalysts.

Q: Which of the following gases is used in the Haber process?
A) HCl
B) N₂
C) NH₃
D) NO₂
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: The Haber process uses nitrogen (N₂) and hydrogen (H₂) to produce ammonia.

Q: When ΔG is negative, the reaction is
A) Non-spontaneous
B) favour forward reaction only
C) favour backward reaction only
D) spontaneous
ANSWER: D
EXPLANATION: A negative value of Gibbs free energy change (ΔG < 0) indicates a spontaneous reaction.

Q: The pH of a solution of 0.001 M HCl is
A) 11
B) 14
C) 12
D) 3
ANSWER: D
EXPLANATION: For strong acid, [H⁺] = 0.001 = 10⁻³ → pH = 3.

Q: Which of the following acids is present in vinegar?

D) citric acid
ANSWER: (Actually ethanoic/acetic acid; closest among options may vary)

Q: Increasing the concentration of the reactant in an equilibrium reaction will
A) lower the equilibrium constant
B) favour the product
C) favour the reactant
D) increase the equilibrium constant
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: By Le Chatelier’s principle, increasing reactant concentration shifts the equilibrium to the right (product side). K remains constant.

Q: Diagonal relationship is shown by

D) B and Si

Q: Eutrophication of water bodies is mainly caused by
A) warm water affluent
B) inorganic fertilizers
C) oxygen gas
D) biodegradable waste
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: Excess inorganic fertilizers (nitrates and phosphates) cause algal bloom leading to eutrophication.

Q: Temporary hardness of water is caused by the presence of

Q: Iron produced directly from the blast furnace is

Q: Polar covalent bond is formed by
A) unequal sharing of electron pair
B) equal sharing of electron pair
C) equal sharing of electron lone pair
D) unequal sharing of electron lone pair
ANSWER: A
EXPLANATION: A polar covalent bond results from the unequal sharing of a bonding electron pair between two atoms of different electronegativity.
