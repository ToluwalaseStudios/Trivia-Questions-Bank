UTME BIOLOGY – SET 2
(Exam-focused)
Each question is independent and ready for separate upload.

Q: What is the powerhouse of the cell?
A) Nucleus
B) Golgi apparatus
C) Mitochondria
D) Endoplasmic reticulum
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: The mitochondrion is the site of cellular respiration where most ATP is produced.

Q: Which organelle is responsible for protein synthesis?
A) Golgi apparatus
B) Ribosome
C) Lysosome
D) Peroxisome
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: Ribosomes are the sites where amino acids are assembled into proteins.

Q: What is the basic unit of heredity?
A) Protein
B) Gene
C) Enzyme
D) Carbohydrate
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: A gene is a segment of DNA that codes for a specific trait and is the basic unit of inheritance.

Q: What is the process by which green plants make their food?
A) Photosynthesis
B) Respiration
C) Digestion
D) Fermentation
ANSWER: A
EXPLANATION: Photosynthesis converts carbon dioxide and water into glucose using light energy and chlorophyll.

Q: Which of the following is not a function of the liver?
A) Detoxification
B) Carbohydrate storage
C) Protein synthesis
D) Hormone secretion
ANSWER: D
EXPLANATION: The liver detoxifies, stores glycogen and synthesises proteins, but major hormone secretion is done by endocrine glands.

Q: Which blood type is known as the universal donor?
A) Type A
B) Type AB
C) Type O
D) Type B
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: Type O blood has no A or B antigens and can be safely given to any ABO blood group.

Q: Which hormone is responsible for regulating blood sugar levels?
A) Insulin
B) Estrogen
C) Testosterone
D) Thyroxine
ANSWER: A
EXPLANATION: Insulin lowers blood glucose by promoting its uptake into cells.

Q: Which of the following is an example of a renewable resource?
A) Coal
B) Natural gas
C) Wind
D) Petroleum
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: Wind is continuously available and can be harnessed without being depleted.

Q: Which of the following is a greenhouse gas?
A) Nitrogen
B) Oxygen
C) Carbon dioxide
D) Hydrogen
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: Carbon dioxide traps heat in the atmosphere and is a major greenhouse gas.

Q: What is the primary function of the respiratory system?
A) Transporting oxygen
B) Excreting waste
C) Breathing
D) Digesting food
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: The primary function is gas exchange (breathing) – taking in oxygen and releasing carbon dioxide.

Q: Which gas is produced during cellular respiration?
A) Oxygen
B) Carbon dioxide
C) Nitrogen
D) Hydrogen
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: Cellular respiration produces carbon dioxide as a waste product.

Q: Which part of the brain controls balance and coordination?
A) Cerebrum
B) Cerebellum
C) Brainstem
D) Hypothalamus
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: The cerebellum coordinates voluntary movements and maintains balance and posture.

Q: What is the largest organ in the human body?
A) Liver
B) Brain
C) Skin
D) Heart
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: The skin is the largest organ by surface area and weight.

Q: Which of the following is not a function of the skeletal system?
A) Protecting internal organs
B) Producing blood cells
C) Controlling body temperature
D) Producing hormones
ANSWER: D
EXPLANATION: The skeletal system supports, protects, produces blood cells and stores minerals; hormone production is mainly endocrine.

Q: What is the function of white blood cells?
A) Transporting oxygen
B) Fighting infection
C) Carrying nutrients
D) Clotting blood
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: White blood cells are the main cells of the immune system that defend the body against pathogens.

Q: What is the purpose of the circulatory system?
A) Transportation of gases, nutrients, and waste products
B) Maintaining body temperature
C) Regulating hormones
D) Providing structural support
ANSWER: A
EXPLANATION: The circulatory system transports oxygen, nutrients, hormones and waste products around the body.

Q: What is the role of DNA in cells?
A) Providing energy
B) Storing genetic information
C) Breaking down waste
D) Regulating cell growth
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: DNA stores the genetic instructions needed for the development and functioning of living organisms.

Q: What is the process by which plants release water vapor into the atmosphere?
A) Transpiration
B) Respiration
C) Photosynthesis
D) Germination
ANSWER: A
EXPLANATION: Transpiration is the loss of water vapour mainly through the stomata of leaves.

Q: What is the process by which cells divide to produce new cells for growth?
A) Replication
B) Mitosis
C) Meiosis
D) Fertilization
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: Mitosis produces two genetically identical daughter cells for growth and repair.

Q: What is the main function of the kidneys?
A) Regulating blood sugar levels
B) Filtering waste from the blood
C) Producing hormones
D) Digesting food
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: The kidneys filter blood to remove urea and other waste products, forming urine.
