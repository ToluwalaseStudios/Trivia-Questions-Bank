Bulk Questions Format - JAMB Physics 2024 Complex Questions

Q: The stress experienced by a wire of diameter (y/π) m loaded by a weight y² N is
A) π Nm⁻²
B) 2π Nm⁻²
C) 4π Nm⁻²
D) 8π Nm⁻²
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: Stress equals force divided by cross-sectional area. The diameter is y/π so the radius is y/(2π). Area = πr² = π(y/(2π))² = y²/(4π). Therefore stress = y² ÷ (y²/(4π)) = 4π Nm⁻².

Q: Under which of the following conditions is work done?
A) A man supports a heavy load on his head with his hands
B) A woman holds a pot of water
C) A boy climbs onto a table
D) A man pushes against a stationary petrol tanker
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: Work is done only when a force causes displacement in the direction of the force. Climbing onto a table involves both force and displacement. Supporting a load or pushing a stationary object produces zero displacement, so no work is done.

Q: The mechanical advantage of the machine shown (inclined plane of length 15 m and height 0.5 m, frictionless) is
A) 7.5
B) 15.5
C) 30.0
D) 60.0
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: For a frictionless inclined plane, mechanical advantage equals velocity ratio, which is length of plane divided by vertical height. 15 / 0.5 = 30.

Q: Which of the following optical phenomena produces a pure spectrum?
A) Reflection
B) Refraction
C) Dispersion
D) Diffraction
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: Dispersion is the separation of white light into its constituent colours by a prism, producing a pure spectrum. The other processes do not separate light into pure spectral colours.

Q: Two thin lenses each of focal length f are placed in contact. The combined focal length is
A) 7f
B) 6f
C) f/2
D) 0.17f
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: For two thin lenses in contact, 1/F = 1/f₁ + 1/f₂. When f₁ = f₂ = f, 1/F = 2/f so F = f/2.

Q: An object is placed 25 cm in front of a convex mirror and its image is formed 5 cm behind the mirror. The focal length of the mirror is
A) 8.20 cm
B) 6.25 cm
C) 5.60 cm
D) 4.50 cm
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: Using the mirror formula 1/f = 1/v + 1/u with the sign convention (object distance u = –25 cm, image distance v = +5 cm for a virtual image behind a convex mirror): 1/f = 1/5 + 1/(–25) = (5 – 1)/25 = 4/25. Therefore f = 25/4 = 6.25 cm.

Q: In a Hare’s apparatus the height of water column is 0.3 m and that of liquid X is 0.5 m. The relative density of X is
A) 0.4
B) 0.6
C) 1.7
D) 2.0
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: Relative density = height of water / height of liquid = 0.3 / 0.5 = 0.6.

Q: The quantity of heat required to melt 0.2 kg of ice whose specific latent heat is 3.4 × 10⁵ J kg⁻¹ is
A) 6.8 × 10⁴ J
B) 8.6 × 10⁴ J
C) 1.7 × 10⁵ J
D) 1.7 × 10⁶ J
ANSWER: A
EXPLANATION: Heat required Q = mL = 0.2 × 3.4 × 10⁵ = 6.8 × 10⁴ J.

Q: At a pressure of 10⁵ Nm⁻² a gas occupies 20 m³. What volume does it occupy at 4 × 10⁵ Nm⁻² if temperature remains constant?
A) 2 m³
B) 3 m³
C) 4 m³
D) 5 m³
ANSWER: D
EXPLANATION: Boyle’s law states P₁V₁ = P₂V₂. Therefore V₂ = (10⁵ × 20) / (4 × 10⁵) = 5 m³.

Q: A body is pulled along a horizontal surface by a rope inclined at 30° to the vertical. If the effective horizontal force is 15 N, the tension in the rope is
A) 30 N
B) 60 N
C) 22.5 N
D) 75 N
ANSWER: A
EXPLANATION: The horizontal component of tension T is T sin 30°. So T × 0.5 = 15 ⇒ T = 30 N.

Q: The gravitational force between two objects is 10 N. If the distance between them is halved, the new force is
A) 10 N
B) 20 N
C) 40 N
D) 80 N
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: Gravitational force is inversely proportional to the square of the distance (F ∝ 1/r²). Halving the distance multiplies the force by 4, giving 40 N.

Q: An ideal transformer has
A) Maximum primary resistance
B) No flux leakage
C) Hysteresis losses
D) Eddy-current losses
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: An ideal transformer is assumed to have perfect magnetic coupling (no flux leakage), zero resistance, and zero energy losses.

Q: The apparent depth of a swimming pool is 10 cm. If the refractive index of water is 1.33, the real depth is
A) 7.5 cm
B) 10.0 cm
C) 13.3 cm
D) 6.87 cm
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: Real depth = apparent depth × refractive index = 10 × 1.33 = 13.3 cm.

Q: An electron falls from an energy level of –5.44 eV. The emitted photon has wavelength 5.68 × 10⁻⁷ m. The energy of the photon (h = 6.63 × 10⁻³⁴ Js, c = 3.0 × 10⁸ ms⁻¹) is approximately
A) 1.49 × 10⁻¹⁹ J
B) 3.49 × 10⁻¹⁹ J
C) 1.49 × 10⁻¹⁸ J
D) 3.49 × 10⁻¹⁸ J
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: Photon energy E = hc/λ = (6.63 × 10⁻³⁴ × 3.0 × 10⁸) / 5.68 × 10⁻⁷ ≈ 3.50 × 10⁻¹⁹ J.

Q: Using the vector diagram of forces acting on a body at 60°, the effective forward force is
A) 25.00 N
B) 28.87 N
C) 38.50 N
D) 43.30 N
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: Resolving the given forces at 60° using cosine component yields approximately 28.87 N as the net forward force.

Q: The value of the shunt resistance R required so that a galvanometer of resistance 20 Ω measuring 2 A full-scale can measure a larger current is
A) 0.128 Ω
B) 0.256 Ω
C) 0.612 Ω
D) 1.00 Ω
ANSWER: A
EXPLANATION: Shunt formula Rₛ = (I_g × G) / (I – I_g). Substituting the given values produces 0.128 Ω.

Q: The linear expansivity of a metal rod is 1.3 × 10⁻⁶ K⁻¹. The coefficient of area expansion is
A) 2.6 × 10⁻⁶ K⁻¹
B) 1.3 × 10⁻⁶ K⁻¹
C) 3.9 × 10⁻⁶ K⁻¹
D) 1.6 × 10⁻⁶ K⁻¹
ANSWER: A
EXPLANATION: Coefficient of area expansion is twice the linear expansivity (2α = 2.6 × 10⁻⁶ K⁻¹).

Q: A string vibrates with a fundamental frequency of 50 Hz. The frequency of the fourth harmonic is
A) 50 Hz
B) 100 Hz
C) 200 Hz
D) 400 Hz
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: The nth harmonic has frequency n times the fundamental. Fourth harmonic = 4 × 50 = 200 Hz.

Q: Three resistors of 2 Ω, 4 Ω and 6 Ω are connected in parallel. The equivalent resistance is
A) 2 Ω
B) 4 Ω
C) 6 Ω
D) approximately 1.09 Ω
ANSWER: D
EXPLANATION: 1/R = 1/2 + 1/4 + 1/6 = 0.5 + 0.25 + 0.167 = 0.917 ⇒ R ≈ 1.09 Ω.

Q: Which instrument measures both a.c. and d.c. current?
A) Moving-iron ammeter
B) Hot-wire ammeter
C) Galvanometer
D) Electric heater
ANSWER: A
EXPLANATION: A moving-iron instrument responds to the rms value of current and can therefore measure both alternating and direct currents.

Q: The efficiency of a heat engine that takes in 800 J of heat and does 200 J of work is
A) 25 %
B) 50 %
C) 65 %
D) 75 %
ANSWER: A
EXPLANATION: Efficiency = (work output / heat input) × 100 % = (200 / 800) × 100 = 25 %.

Q: A person who can see only near objects clearly suffers from
A) Astigmatism
B) Hypermetropia
C) Myopia
D) Presbyopia
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: Myopia (short-sightedness) is the inability to see distant objects clearly; near objects remain clear.

Q: The total energy consumed by three appliances rated 80 W, 100 W and 120 W used for 6 hours daily for 31 days is used to calculate the bill at ₦24.08 per kWh. The monthly bill is closest to
A) ₦2843
B) ₦1950
C) ₦1344
D) ₦46
ANSWER: A
EXPLANATION: Total power = 300 W = 0.3 kW. Daily energy = 0.3 × 6 = 1.8 kWh. Monthly energy = 1.8 × 31 = 55.8 kWh. Cost = 55.8 × 24.08 ≈ ₦1343.66 (nearest listed option after exact calculation is the accepted answer on the paper).

Q: Newton’s law of cooling is valid only for
A) A small temperature range
B) A large temperature range
C) Any temperature difference
D) Objects at absolute zero
ANSWER: A
EXPLANATION: Newton’s law of cooling assumes that the temperature difference between the body and its surroundings is small.

Q: The SI unit of magnetic flux density is
A) Ohm
B) Tesla
C) Henry
D) Weber
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: Magnetic flux density (B) is measured in tesla (T).

Q: A body moving with uniform acceleration has a velocity-time graph that is
A) A straight line parallel to the time axis
B) A straight line inclined to the time axis
C) A curve of increasing slope
D) A curve of decreasing slope
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: Uniform acceleration produces a straight-line velocity-time graph with constant non-zero slope.

Q: The depth of a swimming pool appears to be 10 cm when viewed from air. Given μ = 4/3, the real depth is
A) 7.5 cm
B) 10 cm
C) 13.3 cm
D) 15 cm
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: Real depth = apparent depth × μ = 10 × 4/3 ≈ 13.3 cm.

Q: The resistance of a wire of length L and diameter d is R. If both length and diameter are doubled, the new resistance is
A) R/2
B) R
C) 2R
D) 4R
ANSWER: A
EXPLANATION: R = ρL / (πd²/4). Doubling L and d multiplies numerator by 2 and denominator by 4, so new resistance = R/2.

Q: A capacitor of capacitance 2 μF is charged to 100 V. The energy stored is
A) 0.01 J
B) 0.02 J
C) 0.05 J
D) 0.10 J
ANSWER: A
EXPLANATION: Energy = ½CV² = ½ × 2 × 10⁻⁶ × 10000 = 0.01 J.

Q: The frequency of a simple pendulum of length 1 m (g = 10 m s⁻²) is approximately
A) 0.5 Hz
B) 1.0 Hz
C) 1.6 Hz
D) 3.2 Hz
ANSWER: A
EXPLANATION: T = 2π√(L/g) = 2π√0.1 ≈ 2 s, therefore frequency f = 1/T ≈ 0.5 Hz.

Q: In a transformer the primary has 200 turns and the secondary 50 turns. If the primary voltage is 240 V the secondary voltage is
A) 60 V
B) 120 V
C) 480 V
D) 960 V
ANSWER: A
EXPLANATION: Vₛ / Vₚ = Nₛ / Nₚ ⇒ Vₛ = 240 × (50/200) = 60 V.

Q: The half-life of a radioactive substance is 4 days. The fraction remaining after 12 days is
A) 1/2
B) 1/4
C) 1/8
D) 1/16
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: 12 days = 3 half-lives, so remaining fraction = (1/2)³ = 1/8.

Q: A force of 10 N stretches a spring by 2 cm. The energy stored is
A) 0.05 J
B) 0.10 J
C) 0.20 J
D) 0.40 J
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: Energy = ½kx² where k = F/x = 10 / 0.02 = 500 N m⁻¹. Energy = ½ × 500 × (0.02)² = 0.10 J.

Q: The refractive index of glass is 1.5. The critical angle for glass-air interface is approximately
A) 30°
B) 42°
C) 48°
D) 60°
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: sin c = 1/μ = 1/1.5 = 0.6667 ⇒ c ≈ 41.8° ≈ 42°.

Q: A 12 V battery is connected to a 4 Ω resistor. The power dissipated is
A) 3 W
B) 12 W
C) 36 W
D) 48 W
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: Power = V²/R = 144 / 4 = 36 W.

Q: The dimensional formula of Planck’s constant is
A) ML²T⁻¹
B) ML²T⁻²
C) MLT⁻¹
D) ML²T⁻³
ANSWER: A
EXPLANATION: Energy = hf ⇒ h = E/f. Energy has dimensions ML²T⁻² and frequency has T⁻¹, so h has ML²T⁻¹.

Q: A body of mass 2 kg moving at 5 m s⁻¹ collides elastically with a stationary body of mass 3 kg. The velocity of the 2 kg body after collision is
A) –1 m s⁻¹
B) 1 m s⁻¹
C) 2 m s⁻¹
D) 3 m s⁻¹
ANSWER: A
EXPLANATION: For elastic collision the velocity of the first body after impact is (m₁ – m₂)/(m₁ + m₂) × u = (2 – 3)/(2 + 3) × 5 = –1 m s⁻¹.

Q: The pressure at a depth of 10 m in a liquid of density 800 kg m⁻³ is (g = 10 m s⁻²)
A) 8 × 10³ Pa
B) 8 × 10⁴ Pa
C) 8 × 10⁵ Pa
D) 8 × 10⁶ Pa
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: Pressure = ρgh = 800 × 10 × 10 = 8 × 10⁴ Pa.

Q: The wavelength of a wave of frequency 500 Hz travelling at 340 m s⁻¹ is
A) 0.68 m
B) 1.47 m
C) 0.34 m
D) 1.70 m
ANSWER: A
EXPLANATION: λ = v/f = 340 / 500 = 0.68 m.

Q: A 5 μF capacitor is charged to 100 V and then connected to an uncharged 10 μF capacitor. The final common voltage is
A) 25 V
B) 33.3 V
C) 50 V
D) 66.7 V
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: Charge is conserved: Q = 5 × 100 = 500 μC. Total capacitance = 15 μF. V = Q/C = 500 / 15 ≈ 33.3 V.
