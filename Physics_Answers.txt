Q: The half life of a radioactive substance is 5 days. What percentage of it will remain after 20 days?
A) 12.5%
B) 25%
C) 6.25%
D) 42%
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: Number of half-lives = 20/5 = 4. Remaining fraction = (1/2)^4 = 1/16 = 0.0625 = 6.25%.

Q: Which of the following is true for a coin rolling down an inclined plane?
A) the motion of its centre is rotational while that of any other point in the coin is translational
B) the motion of the centre and that of any other point on the coin is translational
C) the motion of its centre is translational while that of any other point on the coin is rotational
D) the motion of its centre is rotational while that of any other point on the coin is rotational
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: The centre of mass of a rolling coin moves in a straight line (translational motion). Every other point on the coin rotates about the centre, so their motion is rotational (or a combination of rotation and translation).

Q: The energy stored in an elastic material when a force of 1000 N causes an extension of 1 cm is
A) 5 J
B) 10 J
C) 50 J
D) 100 J
ANSWER: A
EXPLANATION: Elastic potential energy = (1/2) × F × e. Convert 1 cm = 0.01 m. Energy = ½ × 1000 × 0.01 = 5 J.

Q: What is the reading on the Kelvin scale when the reading on the Fahrenheit scale is 188°F?
A) 359.7 K
B) 273 K
C) 90 K
D) 156 K
ANSWER: A
EXPLANATION: Formula: K = (°F − 32) × 5/9 + 273. (188 − 32) × 5/9 = 156 × 5/9 = 86.67. Then 86.67 + 273 ≈ 359.7 K.

Q: Given that Mˣ Lʸ Tᶻ is the dimension for G (universal gravitational constant), the respective values of x, y and z are
A) −1, 3, −2
B) 1, −3, 2
C) −1, −3, 2
D) 1, 3, −2
ANSWER: A
EXPLANATION: From F = GMm/r² → G = Fr²/Mm. Dimensions: [F] = MLT⁻², [r²] = L², [M] = M. Therefore [G] = (MLT⁻² × L²)/(M × M) = M⁻¹ L³ T⁻². So x = −1, y = 3, z = −2.

Q: A load of 20 N on a wire of cross-sectional area 8 × 10⁻⁷ m² produces an extension of 10⁻⁴ m. If the length of the wire is 3 m, the Young’s modulus of the material is
A) 7.5 × 10¹¹ N m⁻²
B) 8.5 × 10¹¹ N m⁻²
C) 9.0 × 10¹¹ N m⁻²
D) 7.0 × 10¹¹ N m⁻²
ANSWER: A
EXPLANATION: Y = (F/A) ÷ (e/l) = (F × l)/(A × e). Y = (20 × 3) / (8 × 10⁻⁷ × 10⁻⁴) = 60 / 8 × 10⁻¹¹ = 7.5 × 10¹¹ N m⁻².

Q: The amount of work required to move 500 μC of charge from point A to point B is 0.05 J. The potential difference between A and B is
A) 100 V
B) 50 V
C) 200 V
D) 500 V
ANSWER: A
EXPLANATION: V = W/Q. Q = 500 × 10⁻⁶ C = 5 × 10⁻⁴ C. V = 0.05 / 5 × 10⁻⁴ = 100 V.

Q: A 14 kg body is released from rest on a smooth inclined plane. The angle between the plane and the horizontal is 60°. The acceleration of the body down the plane is (g = 10 m s⁻²)
A) 10 m s⁻²
B) 5 m s⁻²

EXPLANATION: a = g sin θ = 10 × sin 60° = 10 × (√3/2) ≈ 10 × 0.866 = 8.66 m s⁻².

Q: Which of the following is used for the construction of a searchlight?

Q: The force of repulsion between two point positive charges 4 μC and 5 μC that are 0.02 m apart is
A) 9 × 10² N
B) 4.5 × 10² N
C) 1.8 × 10³ N
D) 2.7 × 10³ N
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: F = kq₁q₂/r². k = 9 × 10⁹. F = 9 × 10⁹ × (4 × 10⁻⁶) × (5 × 10⁻⁶) / (0.02)² = 9 × 10⁹ × 20 × 10⁻¹² / 4 × 10⁻⁴ = 180 × 10⁻³ / 4 × 10⁻⁴ = 0.18 / 0.0004 = 450 N = 4.5 × 10² N.

Q: An ebonite rod rubbed with fur attracts a glass rod rubbed with silk because
A) ebonite has a negative charge while glass has a positive charge
B) ebonite has a positive charge while glass has a negative charge
C) both have negative charges
D) both have positive charges
ANSWER: A
EXPLANATION: Fur gives electrons to ebonite (negative), silk removes electrons from glass (positive). Opposite charges attract.

Q: The iron core of an induction coil is made from bundles of wires so as to
A) prevent sparking
B) obtain high secondary voltage
C) minimize eddy currents
D) generate eddy currents
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: Laminated (bundled) cores increase resistance to eddy currents and thereby reduce energy losses.

Q: When a metallic surface is illuminated by light of a given frequency and intensity, it is true that
A) both the number and energy of emitted photoelectrons depend on the frequency of the light
B) both the number and energy of the emitted photoelectrons depend on the intensity of the light
C) the number of the emitted photoelectrons depends on the intensity of the light while the energy depends on the frequency of the light
D) the number of the emitted photoelectrons depends on the frequency while the energy depends on the intensity
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: Intensity determines the number of photons (hence number of photoelectrons). Frequency determines the energy of each photon (hence kinetic energy of photoelectrons).

Q: The velocity V of a particle in time t is given by V = 5t² + 11. The instantaneous acceleration after 2 seconds is

Q: What effort will be required to lift a load of 150 N using a machine whose velocity ratio and efficiency are respectively 5 and 75%?

Q: A ray of light strikes a plane mirror at an angle of incidence 30°. If the mirror is rotated through 40°, through what angle is the reflected ray rotated?
A) 70°
B) 10°
C) 40°
D) 80°
ANSWER: D
EXPLANATION: When a plane mirror is rotated by θ, the reflected ray rotates by 2θ. 2 × 40° = 80°.

Q: What are the first three harmonics of a stretched string of length 2 m if the velocity of the waves is 150 m s⁻¹?
A) 37.5 Hz, 75 Hz, 112.5 Hz
B) 37.5 Hz, 75 Hz, 150 Hz
C) 18.75 Hz, 37.5 Hz, 56.25 Hz
D) 75 Hz, 150 Hz, 225 Hz
ANSWER: A
EXPLANATION: Fundamental f₁ = v/(2L) = 150/(4) = 37.5 Hz. Second harmonic = 2f₁ = 75 Hz. Third = 3f₁ = 112.5 Hz.

Q: A plane sound wave of frequency 85.5 Hz and velocity 343 m s⁻¹ is reflected from a vertical wall. At what distance from the wall does the wave have an antinode?
A) 1 m
B) 2 m
C) 3 m
D) 4 m
ANSWER: A
EXPLANATION: Wavelength λ = v/f = 343/85.5 = 4 m. The first antinode from a rigid wall (displacement node at wall) occurs at λ/4 = 1 m.

Q: The driving mirror of a car has a radius of curvature of 1 m. A vehicle behind the car is 4 m from the mirror. The image distance behind the mirror is
A) 1.143 m
B) 0.571 m
C) 0.444 m
D) 4.5 m
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: For a convex mirror f = +R/2 = +0.5 m. Using 1/v + 1/u = 1/f with u = −4 m: 1/v = 1/0.5 − 1/(−4) = 2 + 0.25 = 2.25 → v = 1/2.25 ≈ 0.444 m.

Q: A submarine is observed to rise from a real depth of 80 m to 60 m in water. The change in apparent depth is (n = 4/3)
A) 15 m
B) 20 m
C) 5 m
D) 10 m
ANSWER: A
EXPLANATION: Apparent depth = real depth / n. Initial apparent = 80 × 3/4 = 60 m. Final apparent = 60 × 3/4 = 45 m. Change = 60 − 45 = 15 m.
