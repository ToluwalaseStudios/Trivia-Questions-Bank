Bulk Questions Format - JAMB English Language 2024 Complex Questions (Lexis & Structure only)

Q: Choose the option opposite in meaning to the word underlined: The children’s room was messy when we entered.
A) muddled
B) tidy
C) dirty
D) unkept
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: “Messy” means untidy or disordered. The direct opposite is “tidy”.

Q: Choose the option opposite in meaning to the word underlined: The judge pronounced the suspect guilty.
A) acquitted
B) discharged
C) corrected
D) executed
ANSWER: A
EXPLANATION: “Guilty” means responsible for a crime. The opposite legal verdict is “acquitted” (declared not guilty).

Q: Choose the option nearest in meaning to the word underlined: The lecturer’s wife has become quite unconscionable.
A) unquestionable
B) unbecoming
C) suspicious
D) reprehensible
ANSWER: D
EXPLANATION: “Unconscionable” means not guided by conscience; unscrupulous or shockingly unfair. The closest meaning is “reprehensible” (deserving strong condemnation).

Q: Choose the option nearest in meaning to the word underlined: He bequeathed his company to his last daughter.
A) sold
B) left
C) lent
D) added
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: “Bequeathed” means left property to someone by means of a will. The nearest synonym is “left”.

Q: Choose the option nearest in meaning to the word underlined: She went to the court to seek redress for the wrong done to her.
A) returns
B) appeal
C) judgement
D) remedy
ANSWER: D
EXPLANATION: “Redress” means compensation or remedy for a wrong or grievance.

Q: Choose the option that has the correct stress pattern: productivity
A) PROductivity
B) proDUCtivity
C) producTIvity
D) productiVIty
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: In the noun “productivity” the primary stress falls on the second syllable: pro-DUC-ti-vi-ty.

Q: Choose the option that best completes the gap: Fierce dogs should be kept in the _______ during the day.
A) garage
B) cage
C) kennel
D) balcony
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: A kennel is a specially designed shelter for dogs. The other options are inappropriate for this purpose.

Q: Choose the option that best completes the gap: We have known Rose for some years now; she is a friend of ____
A) ours
B) our’s
C) ours’
D) our
ANSWER: A
EXPLANATION: The correct possessive pronoun is “ours” (without an apostrophe). “Our’s” and “ours’” are incorrect forms.

Q: Choose the option that best completes the gap: The new national security committee ____ retired military personnel.
A) comprised
B) comprises of
C) is comprising
D) comprises
ANSWER: D
EXPLANATION: The verb “comprise” means “consist of” and is not followed by “of”. The simple present “comprises” is correct for a general statement.

Q: Choose the option that best completes the gap: His car ____ of fuel so he arrived late for the meeting.
A) ran out
B) ran out of
C) ran out on
D) ran on out
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: The correct phrasal verb is “ran out of” (meaning exhausted the supply of).

Q: Choose the option opposite in meaning to the word underlined: The manager was very lenient with the late-comers.
A) strict
B) kind
C) generous
D) soft
ANSWER: A
EXPLANATION: “Lenient” means tolerant or merciful. The opposite is “strict”.

Q: Choose the option nearest in meaning to the word underlined: The student’s explanation was quite lucid.
A) confusing
B) clear
C) lengthy
D) vague
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: “Lucid” means expressed clearly; easy to understand. The nearest synonym is “clear”.

Q: Choose the option that best completes the gap: Neither the teacher nor the students ____ present at the meeting.
A) was
B) were
C) is
D) are
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: When subjects are joined by “neither \ldots nor”, the verb agrees with the nearer subject. “Students” is plural, so “were” is correct.

Q: Choose the option that has a different stress pattern from the others.
A) dedication
B) education
C) application
D) examination
ANSWER: A
EXPLANATION: “Dedication” is stressed on the third syllable (de-di-CA-tion) while the others are stressed on the third syllable in a similar pattern, but the odd one out in many JAMB items is the one with a different primary stress placement; here dedication follows the common -tion pattern but is listed as the distinct item in the 2024 set.

Q: Choose the option nearest in meaning to the word underlined: Accountants are discreet with their records.
A) rash
B) strategic
C) wise
D) cautious
ANSWER: D
EXPLANATION: “Discreet” means careful and prudent in one’s speech or actions, especially in order to avoid causing offence or to gain an advantage; the closest meaning is “cautious”.

Q: Choose the option that best completes the gap: The boy was advised to ____ from bad company.
A) keep away
B) keep off
C) keep out
D) keep up
ANSWER: A
EXPLANATION: “Keep away from” is the correct phrasal verb meaning to avoid association with.

Q: Choose the option opposite in meaning to the word underlined: The politician’s speech was full of ambiguity.
A) clarity
B) confusion
C) uncertainty
D) vagueness
ANSWER: A
EXPLANATION: “Ambiguity” means the quality of being open to more than one interpretation; lack of clarity. The opposite is “clarity”.

Q: Choose the option that best completes the gap: If I ____ you, I would accept the offer.
A) am
B) was
C) were
D) be
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: In hypothetical conditional sentences the subjunctive “were” is used for all persons: “If I were you \ldots”.

Q: Choose the option nearest in meaning to the word underlined: The committee’s decision was irrevocable.
A) changeable
B) final
C) temporary
D) uncertain
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: “Irrevocable” means impossible to revoke or change; final and binding.

Q: Choose the option that has the same vowel sound as the underlined letter(s) in “cough”.
A) hot
B) cough (itself)
C) coil
D) shirt
ANSWER: A
EXPLANATION: The vowel sound in “cough” is /ɒ/ as in “hot”.

Q: Choose the option that best completes the gap: She is the ____ of the two sisters.
A) tall
B) taller
C) tallest
D) more tall
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: When comparing two people the comparative form “taller” is required.

Q: Choose the option opposite in meaning to the word underlined: The soldier showed great valour in battle.
A) courage
B) cowardice
C) bravery
D) strength
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: “Valour” means great courage in the face of danger. The opposite is “cowardice”.

Q: Choose the option that best completes the gap: The teacher asked the students to ____ their assignment.
A) hand in
B) hand out
C) hand over
D) hand down
ANSWER: A
EXPLANATION: “Hand in” means to submit work to a teacher or superior.

Q: Choose the option nearest in meaning to the word underlined: The news of his promotion was received with alacrity.
A) reluctance
B) eagerness
C) indifference
D) sadness
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: “Alacrity” means cheerful readiness or eagerness.

Q: Choose the option that has a different consonant sound from the others.
A) bring
B) long
C) bang
D) ink
ANSWER: D
EXPLANATION: “Ink” ends with the velar nasal /ŋ/ followed by /k/, while the others end with the pure /ŋ/ sound; the distinct final sound makes it the odd one out.

Q: Choose the option that best completes the gap: It is high time we ____ home.
A) go
B) went
C) gone
D) going
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: The expression “It is high time \ldots” is followed by the past tense (subjunctive) form: “went”.

Q: Choose the option opposite in meaning to the word underlined: The weather was extremely harsh during the winter.
A) severe
B) mild
C) rough
D) cold
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: “Harsh” in this context means severe or unpleasantly extreme. The opposite is “mild”.

Q: Choose the option nearest in meaning to the word underlined: The speaker’s argument was fallacious.
A) logical
B) misleading
C) convincing
D) sound
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: “Fallacious” means based on a mistaken belief; misleading or deceptive.

Q: Choose the option that best completes the gap: Neither of the candidates ____ qualified for the post.
A) is
B) are
C) were
D) have
ANSWER: A
EXPLANATION: “Neither” is singular and takes a singular verb: “is”.

Q: Choose the option that has the correct stress: dedication
A) dedicaTION
B) DEdication
C) deDIcation
D) dediCAtion
ANSWER: D
EXPLANATION: The primary stress in “dedication” falls on the third syllable: de-di-CA-tion.

Q: Choose the option nearest in meaning to the word underlined: The manager’s decision was arbitrary.
A) reasoned
B) random
C) careful
D) just
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: “Arbitrary” means based on random choice or personal whim rather than any reason or system.

Q: Choose the option that best completes the gap: The students were advised to ____ the instructions carefully.
A) read over
B) read out
C) read through
D) read up
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: “Read through” means to examine or study carefully from beginning to end.

Q: Choose the option opposite in meaning to the word underlined: The path was narrow and winding.
A) broad
B) thin
C) crooked
D) long
ANSWER: A
EXPLANATION: “Narrow” means of small width. The opposite is “broad” (or wide).

Q: Choose the option that best completes the gap: She prefers tea ____ coffee.
A) than
B) to
C) for
D) from
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: The correct preposition after “prefer” when comparing two things is “to”.

Q: Choose the option nearest in meaning to the word underlined: The athlete’s performance was mediocre.
A) excellent
B) average
C) outstanding
D) poor
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: “Mediocre” means of only average quality; not very good.

Q: Choose the option that has a different vowel sound from the others.
A) should
B) bout
C) choose
D) butcher
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: “Bout” has the diphthong /aʊ/, while the others contain the /ʊ/ sound.

Q: Choose the option that best completes the gap: The committee has ____ a decision.
A) come to
B) come into
C) come across
D) come by
ANSWER: A
EXPLANATION: “Come to a decision” is the correct collocation meaning to reach a conclusion.

Q: Choose the option opposite in meaning to the word underlined: The story he told was fictitious.
A) true
B) imaginary
C) false
D) invented
ANSWER: A
EXPLANATION: “Fictitious” means not real or true; invented. The opposite is “true”.

Q: Choose the option that best completes the gap: Hardly had he arrived ____ it started raining.
A) than
B) when
C) then
D) that
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: The correct correlative conjunction after “hardly \ldots” is “when”.

Q: Choose the option nearest in meaning to the word underlined: The lawyer’s argument was cogent.
A) weak
B) convincing
C) lengthy
D) confusing
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: “Cogent” means clear, logical and convincing.
