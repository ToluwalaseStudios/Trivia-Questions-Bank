Q1.
Question: What is the main product formed during the complete combustion of a hydrocarbon in an ample supply of oxygen?
A. Carbon monoxide and water
B. Carbon dioxide and water
C. Carbon dioxide and hydrogen gas
D. Carbon monoxide and hydrogen gas
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Complete combustion of any hydrocarbon yields carbon dioxide ($CO_2$) and water ($H_2O$) as the primary products when sufficient oxygen is available.

Q2.
Question: Which subatomic particle determines the atomic number of an element?
A. Neutron
B. Electron
C. Proton
D. Positron
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: The atomic number ($Z$) of an element is strictly defined by the number of protons found in the nucleus of its atoms.

Q3.
Question: What type of chemical bond involves the equal sharing of electron pairs between atoms?
A. Ionic bond
B. Polar covalent bond
C. Nonpolar covalent bond
D. Metallic bond
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Nonpolar covalent bonds occur when two atoms share electrons equally due to having identical or very similar electronegativities.

Q4.
Question: What is the pH of a neutral aqueous solution at 25 °C?
A. 0
B. 7
C. 14
D. 1
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: At 25 °C, a neutral solution has equal concentrations of hydrogen ions $[H^+]$ and hydroxide ions $[OH^-]$, resulting in a pH of exactly 7.

Q5.
Question: Which law states that at constant temperature, the volume of a fixed mass of gas is inversely proportional to its pressure?
A. Charles's Law
B. Gay-Lussac's Law
C. Boyle's Law
D. Avogadro's Law
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Boyle's Law specifies that $P_1 V_1 = P_2 V_2$ for a fixed amount of gas at constant temperature, showing an inverse relation between pressure and volume.

Q6.
Question: What is the oxidation state of sulfur in sulfuric acid ($H_2SO_4$)?
A. +2
B. +4
C. +6
D. +8
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Hydrogen has an oxidation state of +1 (total +2 for two H) and oxygen has -2 (total -8 for four O). To maintain electrical neutrality, sulfur must be +6.

Q7.
Question: Which functional group characterizes alcohols?
A. -COOH
B. -CHO
C. -OH
D. -NH2
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Alcohols contain a hydroxyl functional group (-OH) attached to a saturated carbon atom.

Q8.
Question: What element has the highest electronegativity on the Pauling scale?
A. Oxygen
B. Chlorine
C. Fluorine
D. Nitrogen
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Fluorine is the most electronegative element, assigned a value of approximately 3.98 on the Pauling scale.

Q9.
Question: Which state of matter has a definite volume but no definite shape?
A. Solid
B. Liquid
C. Gas
D. Plasma
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Liquids take the shape of their containers while maintaining a fixed, definite volume.

Q10.
Question: What is the molar mass of water ($H_2O$) rounded to two decimal places?
A. 16.00 g/mol
B. 18.02 g/mol
C. 20.02 g/mol
D. 17.01 g/mol
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Molar mass of $H_2O = 2 \times (1.008 \text{ g/mol}) + 1 \times (15.999 \text{ g/mol}) \approx 18.02 \text{ g/mol}$.

Q11.
Question: In a redox reaction, what happens to the substance that undergoes reduction?
A. It gains electrons and its oxidation number increases.
B. It loses electrons and its oxidation number increases.
C. It gains electrons and its oxidation number decreases.
D. It loses electrons and its oxidation number decreases.
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Reduction corresponds to the gain of electrons (OIL RIG: Oxidation Is Loss, Reduction Is Gain), which lowers (reduces) the oxidation state.

Q12.
Question: What principle states that no two electrons in an atom can have the same set of four quantum numbers?
A. Hund's Rule
B. Aufbau Principle
C. Pauli Exclusion Principle
D. Heisenberg Uncertainty Principle
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: The Pauli Exclusion Principle asserts that an orbital can hold a maximum of two electrons, and they must have opposite spins.

Q13.
Question: Which of the following is an intensive property of matter?
A. Mass
B. Density
C. Volume
D. Enthalpy
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Density is an intensive property because it does not depend on the amount of matter present, unlike mass, volume, or enthalpy.

Q14.
Question: What phase change occurs when a substance transitions directly from a gas to a solid?
A. Sublimation
B. Deposition
C. Condensation
D. Freezing
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Deposition is the thermodynamic process in which gas transforms directly into a solid without passing through the liquid phase.

Q15.
Question: What is the SI unit for measuring the amount of a substance?
A. Gram
B. Liter
C. Mole
D. Dalton
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: The mole (mol) is the official SI base unit used to measure the quantity of chemical entities (atoms, molecules, ions).

Q16.
Question: According to Le Chatelier's principle, what happens if pressure is increased in a gaseous equilibrium system?
A. The reaction shifts toward the side with fewer moles of gas.
B. The reaction shifts toward the side with more moles of gas.
C. The equilibrium constant increases.
D. No change occurs in system position.
Correct Answer: A
Explanation: Increasing pressure forces the equilibrium to shift in the direction that produces fewer gas molecules to relieve the applied pressure.

Q17.
Question: What is the conjugate base of the acid $HCO_3^-$?
A. $H_2CO_3$
B. $CO_3^{2-}$
C. $OH^-$
D. $H_3O^+$
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: A conjugate base is formed when an acid loses a proton ($H^+$). Removing $H^+$ from $HCO_3^-$ yields the carbonate ion, $CO_3^{2-}$.

Q18.
Question: What type of reaction is $2Mg + O_2 \rightarrow 2MgO$?
A. Decomposition
B. Single replacement
C. Synthesis
D. Double replacement
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: A synthesis (or combination) reaction occurs when two or more simple reactants combine to form a single complex product.

Q19.
Question: Which subatomic particle has a relative charge of -1 and negligible mass compared to nucleons?
A. Proton
B. Neutron
C. Electron
D. Alpha particle
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Electrons carry a charge of $-1.6 \times 10^{-19}\text{ C}$ (relative charge -1) and have a mass roughly $1/1836$ that of a proton.

Q20.
Question: Which element has the electron configuration $1s^2 2s^2 2p^6 3s^2 3p^6$?
A. Neon
B. Argon
C. Krypton
D. Chlorine
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: The electron configuration accounts for a total of 18 electrons, corresponding to the noble gas Argon ($Z = 18$).

Q21.
Question: What term describes a solution that contains the maximum amount of dissolved solute at equilibrium at a specific temperature?
A. Unsaturated
B. Saturated
C. Supersaturated
D. Dilute
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: A saturated solution holds the maximum concentration of solute dissolved in the solvent at a given temperature and pressure.

Q22.
Question: What is the geometry of a methane ($CH_4$) molecule according to VSEPR theory?
A. Trigonal planar
B. Linear
C. Tetrahedral
D. Bent
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Methane has four bonding pairs and zero lone pairs around the central carbon atom, resulting in a tetrahedral geometric arrangement.

Q23.
Question: Which catalyst is used in the industrial Haber-Bosch process for ammonia synthesis?
A. Platinum
B. Nickel
C. Iron
D. Vanadium(V) oxide
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Finely divided iron (often promoted with potassium and aluminum oxides) is used as the catalyst in the Haber-Bosch process.

Q24.
Question: What is the formula for calculating molarity ($M$)?
A. Moles of solute / Kilograms of solvent
B. Grams of solute / Liters of solution
C. Moles of solute / Liters of solution
D. Moles of solvent / Liters of solution
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Molarity is defined as the number of moles of solute dissolved per liter of solution ($\text{mol/L}$).

Q25.
Question: Which halogen exists as a reddish-brown liquid at room temperature and standard pressure?
A. Fluorine
B. Chlorine
C. Bromine
D. Iodine
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Elemental bromine ($Br_2$) is one of only two elements (along with mercury) that are liquid at standard temperature and pressure.

Q26.
Question: What happens to the rate of most chemical reactions when the temperature is increased?
A. It increases because collision frequency and energy increase.
B. It decreases because activation energy increases.
C. It remains constant.
D. It decreases because molecules move slower.
Correct Answer: A
Explanation: Higher temperature increases the kinetic energy of particles, leading to more frequent and energetic collisions that exceed activation energy.

Q27.
Question: Which type of decay emits a helium nucleus ($^4_2He$)?
A. Beta-minus decay
B. Gamma decay
C. Alpha decay
D. Positron emission
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Alpha decay involves the emission of an alpha particle, which consists of 2 protons and 2 neutrons ($^4_2He^{2+}$).

Q28.
Question: What is the name of the organic compound with the molecular formula $C_2H_2$?
A. Ethane
B. Ethene
C. Ethyne
D. Methane
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Ethyne (commonly known as acetylene) contains a carbon-carbon triple bond and has the chemical formula $C_2H_2$.

Q29.
Question: Which quantity remains constant during an isothermal thermodynamic process?
A. Pressure
B. Volume
C. Temperature
D. Heat capacity
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: An isothermal process is a change of a system in which the temperature remains constant ($\Delta T = 0$).

Q30.
Question: What bond angle is associated with $sp^2$ hybridized orbitals?
A. 180°
B. 109.5°
C. 120°
D. 90°
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: $sp^2$ hybridization leads to a trigonal planar electron geometry with ideal bond angles of 120°.

Q31.
Question: What is the term for a substance that can act as both an acid and a base?
A. Amphiprotic
B. Aprotic
C. Anhydrous
D. Isomorphic
Correct Answer: A
Explanation: Amphiprotic (or more broadly, amphoteric) substances can either donate or accept protons depending on the reaction conditions.

Q32.
Question: Which law states that total enthalpy change for a chemical reaction is independent of the pathway taken?
A. Raoult's Law
B. Hess's Law
C. Henry's Law
D. Dalton's Law
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Hess's Law states that the enthalpy change of a reaction depends only on the initial and final states, not the pathway.

Q33.
Question: What gas is evolved when a metal carbonate reacts with an acid?
A. Hydrogen gas
B. Oxygen gas
C. Carbon dioxide gas
D. Nitrogen dioxide gas
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Metal carbonates react with acids to yield a salt, water, and carbon dioxide gas ($CO_2$).

Q34.
Question: What is the main component of natural gas?
A. Ethane
B. Propane
C. Methane
D. Butane
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Methane ($CH_4$) accounts for roughly 70% to 90% of the composition of natural gas.

Q35.
Question: What is the term for a mixture that maintains a constant boiling point and composition throughout distillation?
A. Isotope
B. Azeotrope
C. Suspension
D. Colloid
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: An azeotrope is a mixture of liquids whose proportion cannot be altered or changed by simple distillation.

Q36.
Question: Which quantum number describes the shape of an atomic orbital?
A. Principal quantum number ($n$)
B. Azimuthal quantum number ($l$)
C. Magnetic quantum number ($m_l$)
D. Spin quantum number ($m_s$)
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: The azimuthal (or orbital angular momentum) quantum number $l$ determines the subshell and shape of the orbital.

Q37.
Question: What color does a strong acid turn blue litmus paper?
A. Red
B. Green
C. Yellow
D. Colorless
Correct Answer: A
Explanation: Acids turn blue litmus paper red, while bases turn red litmus paper blue.

Q38.
Question: What is the name of the process by which an unstable atomic nucleus loses energy by radiation?
A. Nuclear fusion
B. Radioactive decay
C. Ionization
D. Electrophoresis
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Radioactive decay is the spontaneous process by which an unstable nucleus releases particles or electromagnetic radiation.

Q39.
Question: Which property describes a liquid's resistance to flow?
A. Surface tension
B. Capillarity
C. Viscosity
D. Vapor pressure
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Viscosity measures a fluid's internal resistance to flow; higher viscosity means thicker, slower-flowing liquids.

Q40.
Question: What is the coordination number of an atom in a face-centered cubic (FCC) unit cell?
A. 6
B. 8
C. 12
D. 14
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: In a face-centered cubic (FCC) lattice structure, each atom touches 12 nearest neighbors, giving it a coordination number of 12.
