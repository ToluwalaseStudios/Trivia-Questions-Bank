Q1.
Question: What is the primary function of the mitochondria in eukaryotic cells?
A. Protein synthesis
B. ATP production via cellular respiration
C. Lipid storage
D. DNA replication
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Mitochondria are known as the powerhouses of the cell because they generate most of the cell's ATP through aerobic respiration.

Q2.
Question: Which organelle is responsible for synthesizing proteins?
A. Ribosome
B. Lysosome
C. Golgi apparatus
D. Endoplasmic reticulum
Correct Answer: A
Explanation: Ribosomes read mRNA sequences and translate them into polypeptide chains during protein synthesis.

Q3.
Question: What is the primary pigment involved in photosynthesis in green plants?
A. Carotenoid
B. Chlorophyll a
C. Anthocyanin
D. Xanthophyll
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Chlorophyll a is the primary photosynthetic pigment that absorbs light energy necessary for converting $CO_2$ and $H_2O$ into glucose.

Q4.
Question: Which molecule serves as the primary energy currency of the cell?
A. DNA
B. RNA
C. ATP
D. NADH
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Adenosine triphosphate (ATP) stores and provides energy for vital cellular processes.

Q5.
Question: What is the process by which cells divide to produce four genetically distinct haploid gametes?
A. Mitosis
B. Binary fission
C. Meiosis
D. Budding
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Meiosis consists of two successive cell divisions that result in four haploid daughter cells with genetic variation.
