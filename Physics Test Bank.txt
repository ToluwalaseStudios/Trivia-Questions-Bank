Q1.
Question: What is Newton's Second Law of Motion?
A. For every action, there is an equal and opposite reaction.
B. An object at rest stays at rest unless acted upon by an external force.
C. Acceleration is directly proportional to net force and inversely proportional to mass ($F = ma$).
D. Gravitational force decreases with the square of the distance.
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Newton's second law states $F_{net} = ma$, relating net force, mass, and acceleration.

Q2.
Question: What is the SI unit of electric current?
A. Volt
B. Ohm
C. Ampere
D. Joule
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: The Ampere (A) is the SI base unit used to measure electric current.

Q3.
Question: Which principle explains the buoyant force acting on an object submerged in a fluid?
A. Pascal's Principle
B. Bernoulli's Principle
C. Archimedes' Principle
D. Hooke's Law
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Archimedes' Principle states that the buoyant force on an object equals the weight of the fluid it displaces.

Q4.
Question: What type of wave requires a physical medium to travel through?
A. Electromagnetic wave
B. Mechanical wave
C. Light wave
D. Radio wave
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Mechanical waves (such as sound waves) require a physical medium to transfer energy, unlike electromagnetic waves.

Q5.
Question: What is the kinetic energy formula for an object of mass $m$ moving at velocity $v$?
A. $KE = m v$
B. $KE = \frac{1}{2} m v^2$
C. $KE = m g h$
D. $KE = \frac{1}{2} m^2 v$
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Kinetic energy is calculated as $KE = \frac{1}{2} m v^2$.
