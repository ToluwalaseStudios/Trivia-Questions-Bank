Q: A car starts from rest and accelerates uniformly at 4 m/s² for 5 seconds. What distance does it cover?
A) 20 m
B) 40 m
C) 50 m
D) 100 m
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: Using s = ut + ½at², with u = 0, a = 4 m/s², t = 5 s: s = ½ × 4 × 5² = 50 m.

Q: A particle moving with an initial velocity of 10 m/s accelerates uniformly at 2 m/s². What will be its velocity after travelling for 15 s?
A) 20 m/s
B) 30 m/s
C) 40 m/s
D) 50 m/s
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: Using v = u + at: v = 10 + (2 × 15) = 40 m/s.

Q: The dimensional formula for pressure is:
A) ML⁻¹T⁻²
B) MLT⁻²
C) ML²T⁻²
D) M⁻¹LT⁻²
ANSWER: A
EXPLANATION: Pressure = Force/Area. Force has dimensions MLT⁻², and area has dimensions L². Dividing gives ML⁻¹T⁻².

Q: Which of the following quantities has the same dimensions as energy?
A) Force
B) Power
C) Torque
D) Pressure
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: Torque = Force × distance, giving dimensions ML²T⁻², which is identical to the dimensions of energy.

Q: A stone is projected horizontally from the top of a cliff. Neglecting air resistance, which statement is correct?
A) Its horizontal velocity continuously decreases
B) Its vertical velocity remains constant
C) Its horizontal velocity remains constant
D) Its acceleration is zero
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: No horizontal force acts on the stone (ignoring air resistance), so horizontal velocity stays constant while vertical velocity increases due to gravity.

Q: At the highest point of the trajectory of a projectile:
A) Both horizontal and vertical velocities are zero
B) Vertical velocity is zero but horizontal velocity is not zero
C) Horizontal velocity is zero but vertical velocity is not zero
D) Both velocity and acceleration are zero
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: At the peak, the vertical component of velocity momentarily equals zero, while the horizontal component remains unchanged throughout the flight.

Q: A body moving in a circle at constant speed is accelerating because:
A) Its speed is increasing
B) Its mass is changing
C) Its direction of motion is continuously changing
D) Its radius is continuously increasing
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: Acceleration is a change in velocity, and velocity is a vector. Even at constant speed, a continuous change in direction means the velocity vector is changing, producing acceleration.

Q: The centripetal force acting on a body moving in a circle is always directed:
A) Tangentially to the circle
B) Away from the centre
C) Towards the centre
D) Opposite to the direction of motion
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: Centripetal force pulls the body toward the centre of the circular path, which is what keeps it moving in a curve instead of a straight line.

Q: Which of the following waves cannot travel through a vacuum?
A) Radio waves
B) Light waves
C) Sound waves
D) X-rays
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: Sound waves are mechanical waves and need a material medium (solid, liquid, or gas) to propagate, unlike electromagnetic waves, which can travel through a vacuum.

Q: A wave has a frequency of 50 Hz and wavelength of 4 m. What is its speed?
A) 12.5 m/s
B) 46 m/s
C) 54 m/s
D) 200 m/s
ANSWER: D
EXPLANATION: Wave speed = frequency × wavelength = 50 × 4 = 200 m/s.
