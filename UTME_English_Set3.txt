========================================
COMPREHENSION / CLOZE STYLE
========================================

Q: Many Nigerians are yet to appreciate the importance of ______ good food.
A) consuming
B) eating
C) swallowing
D) taking
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: “Eating” is the most natural and commonly used collocation with “good food” in this context.

Q: The major reason for this lack of ______ is to be found in the pattern of spending.
A) awareness
B) seriousness
C) sense of responsibility
D) determination
ANSWER: A
EXPLANATION: The passage discusses lack of awareness of the importance of good food.

Q: This pattern is characterised by lack of ______.
A) thinking
B) saving
C) planning
D) controlling
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: The wasteful spending habit points to lack of proper planning.

Q: A good number of whom are quite ______ when it comes to buying clothes.
A) arrogant
B) stingy
C) crazy
D) extravagant
ANSWER: D
EXPLANATION: The context of spending heavily on expensive clothes shows they are extravagant.

Q: This ______ for expensive clothes has been brought about by the belief that it is appearance that ______.
A) desire / produces
B) anticipation / records
C) aversion / determines
D) regret / decides
ANSWER: A
EXPLANATION: Desire for expensive clothes is linked to the belief that appearance produces the type of person one is.

========================================
NEAREST IN MEANING
========================================

Q: The accident victims bled "PROFUSELY".
A) diffusely
B) clearly
C) slowly
D) excessively
ANSWER: D
EXPLANATION: Profusely means in large amounts / excessively.

Q: Although our leader preached "EGALITARIANISM", he could not match it up with action.
A) salvation
B) dedication
C) kindness
D) equality
ANSWER: D
EXPLANATION: Egalitarianism means belief in equality of all people.

Q: My sister married a "DOTING" husband.
A) a fun-loving
B) an uncaring
C) a nagging
D) a loving
ANSWER: D
EXPLANATION: Doting means extremely fond / loving.

Q: Okolo played the piano with great "DEXTERITY".
A) wisdom
B) power
C) force
D) skill
ANSWER: D
EXPLANATION: Dexterity means skill and neatness in using the hands.

Q: They have tried to "CIRCUMVENT" the restriction on the importation of the commodities.
A) bypass
B) confront
C) oppose
D) challenge
ANSWER: A
EXPLANATION: Circumvent means to find a way around / bypass a rule or restriction.

========================================
OPPOSITE IN MEANING
========================================

Q: The chairman’s verdict "REDRESSED" the injustice meted out by the secretary.
A) restored
B) corrected
C) aggravated
D) addressed
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: Redressed means corrected or put right; the opposite is aggravated (made worse).

Q: Sola could not travel yesterday because of the "SEVERITY" of the weather.
A) cold
B) mildness
C) warmth
D) moderation
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: Severity means harshness; the opposite is mildness.

Q: He devoted much time to the "PERIPHERAL" aspect of the topic.
A) unimportant
B) superficial
C) minor
D) main
ANSWER: D
EXPLANATION: Peripheral means minor or of little importance; the opposite is main.

Q: Laide is "IMMUNE" to the flattery of young men.
A) resistant
B) susceptible
C) insensible
D) unresponsive
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: Immune means not affected by; the opposite is susceptible (easily affected).

Q: The chairman advised every member of the group to tolerate "DISPARATE" dispositions.
A) converging
B) accommodating
C) conflicting
D) opposing
ANSWER: A
EXPLANATION: Disparate means different or contrasting; the opposite is converging (coming together / similar).

========================================
LEKKI HEADMASTER
========================================

Q: Who is the protagonist of The Lekki Headmaster?
A) Mrs Ibidun Gloss
B) Mr Adewale Adebepo (Bepo)
C) Mr Audu
D) Chief Ogba
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: Mr Adewale Adebepo, popularly called Bepo, is the central character and principal of Stardom Schools.

Q: What major event opens the novel The Lekki Headmaster?
A) A student election scandal
B) Mr Bepo breaking down in tears at morning assembly
C) The MD announcing a fee increase
D) A parent attacking a teacher
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: The novel begins with the usually confident principal unable to speak and weeping uncontrollably during assembly.

Q: Why was Mr Bepo under intense pressure to leave Nigeria?
A) He had been dismissed from Stardom Schools
B) His wife Seri and children were already in the UK and wanted him to join them
C) He had received a better job offer from another school
D) He wanted to start a commercial transport business
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: Bepo’s wife Seri (a nurse in the UK) and their daughters had already relocated and constantly pressured him to “japa”.

Q: What is the central physical setting of The Lekki Headmaster?
A) Beesway Group of Schools
B) Black Heritage Museum, Badagry
C) Stardom Schools, Lekki, Lagos
D) Murtala Muhammed Airport
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: Almost the entire story unfolds at Stardom Schools located in the affluent Lekki area of Lagos.

Q: What does the term “JAPA” primarily refer to in the novel?
A) A school strike action
B) The mass migration of Nigerians abroad in search of a better life
C) A traditional cultural festival
D) The school’s excursion programme
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: “Japa” describes the contemporary Nigerian phenomenon of relocating abroad for better opportunities.

Q: How much was the boarding-house fee per session at Stardom before it was reduced?
A) One hundred and sixty-three thousand naira
B) Two hundred and fifty thousand naira
C) One hundred and sixty thousand naira
D) Ninety-three thousand naira
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: The novel states that the boarding fee was reduced from ₦250,000 as part of the school’s fee-restructuring strategy.

Q: At the end of the novel, what decision does Mr Bepo finally take?
A) He boards the flight to the UK
B) He resigns and starts his own school immediately
C) He returns to Stardom Schools, declaring that his heart is in Nigeria
D) He accepts a transfer to another branch of the school
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: After the grand farewell, Bepo surprisingly returns and announces that he is not going abroad; his heart and mission remain at Stardom.

Q: Why was Mr Bepo nicknamed “The Lekki Headmaster”?
A) He lived in Lekki during his time as a headmaster
B) He humorously imitated characters from the classic TV drama Village Headmaster
C) He introduced a new curriculum in the school
D) He founded Stardom Kiddies School
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: The nickname stuck because of his humorous imitation of characters from the popular Nigerian television series Village Headmaster while resolving conflicts.

Q: What was Mr Bepo’s monthly salary at Stardom Schools?
A) N200,000
B) N400,000
C) N600,000
D) N1,000,000
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: The novel states that Bepo’s monthly salary as principal was ₦400,000.

Q: What is the main challenge that Mr Bepo faces in the novel?
A) Convincing parents to enrol their children
B) Managing the school’s finances
C) Balancing his career with pressure to migrate
D) Starting his own school
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: The central conflict is Bepo’s internal struggle between remaining committed to his students in Nigeria and the strong family pressure to relocate to the UK.
