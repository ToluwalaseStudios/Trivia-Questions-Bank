Q: Which path does sound entering the human ear follow?
A) Oval window, ossicles, ear drum
B) ear drum, oval window, ossicles
C) Ear drum, ossicles, oval window
D) Ossicles, ear drum, oval window
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: Sound waves entering the ear first strike the eardrum (tympanic membrane), causing it to vibrate; these vibrations are then transmitted and amplified through the chain of ossicles, before finally reaching the oval window leading into the inner ear.

D) on the blind spot

EXPLANATION: As established earlier, myopia (short-sightedness) causes the eye to focus incoming light from distant objects to a point in front of the retina, rather than precisely on it, resulting in a blurred image.

B) positive phototropism
C) positive phototaxism

ANSWER: D
