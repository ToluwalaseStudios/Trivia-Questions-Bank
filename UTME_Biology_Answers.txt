UTME BIOLOGY – ANSWERS & EXPLANATIONS
(Exam-focused)
Each question is independent and ready for separate upload.

Q: Which of the following organisms is a protozoan?
A) Spirogyra
B) Amoeba
C) Volvox
D) Paramecium
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: Amoeba is a unicellular protozoan. Spirogyra and Volvox are green algae; Paramecium is also a protozoan, but the most classic example among the options commonly accepted as correct in many versions is Amoeba. (Note: Both B and D are protozoa; standard answer key selects B.)

Q: The functional unit of the kidney is the:
A) Glomerulus
B) Nephron
C) Bowman’s capsule
D) Loop of Henle
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: The nephron is the basic structural and functional unit of the kidney responsible for filtration, reabsorption and secretion.

Q: Which process is responsible for the movement of water from the soil into the root hairs?
A) Diffusion
B) Active transport
C) Osmosis
D) Transpiration
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: Water moves from the soil (higher water potential) into the root hairs (lower water potential) by osmosis across the selectively permeable cell membrane.

Q: The mode of nutrition in fungi is:
A) Autotrophic
B) Parasitic
C) Saprophytic
D) Holozoic
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: Most fungi are saprophytes; they obtain nutrients by secreting enzymes onto dead organic matter and absorbing the digested products.

Q: Which of the following is NOT a characteristic of mammals?
A) Warm-blooded
B) Lay eggs
C) Possess mammary glands
D) Have hair or fur
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: Most mammals give birth to live young (viviparous). Only a few (monotremes) lay eggs; therefore “lay eggs” is not a general characteristic of mammals.

Q: Which part of the cell is responsible for respiration?
A) Nucleus
B) Mitochondrion
C) Ribosome
D) Lysosome
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: The mitochondrion is the site of cellular respiration where ATP is produced (power house of the cell).

Q: The movement of molecules from a region of higher concentration to a region of lower concentration is known as:
A) Osmosis
B) Translocation
C) Diffusion
D) Active transport
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: Diffusion is the passive movement of molecules down a concentration gradient.

Q: In plants, which hormone promotes elongation of cells?
A) Cytokinin
B) Auxin
C) Gibberellin
D) Abscisic acid
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: Auxins (especially IAA) promote cell elongation, particularly in the shoot.

Q: Which of these is a vector of malaria?
A) Culex mosquito
B) Tsetse fly
C) Anopheles mosquito
D) Black fly
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: The female Anopheles mosquito transmits the Plasmodium parasite that causes malaria.

Q: Which of the following is not a vertebrate?
A) Fish
B) Toad
C) Earthworm
D) Lizard
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: Earthworm is an invertebrate (annelid). Fish, toad and lizard all possess a vertebral column.

Q: The process by which green plants make their food is:
A) Respiration
B) Photosynthesis
C) Digestion
D) Fermentation
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: Photosynthesis is the process by which green plants convert carbon dioxide and water into glucose using sunlight and chlorophyll.

Q: Which structure in the mammalian eye is responsible for focusing light?
A) Iris
B) Retina
C) Lens
D) Cornea
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: The lens changes shape (accommodation) to focus light rays onto the retina.

Q: Which of these diseases is not caused by bacteria?
A) Cholera
B) Tuberculosis
C) Typhoid
D) Ringworm
ANSWER: D
EXPLANATION: Ringworm is a fungal infection. Cholera, tuberculosis and typhoid are bacterial diseases.

Q: The blood vessel that carries blood from the lungs to the heart is the:
A) Pulmonary vein
B) Pulmonary artery
C) Aorta
D) Vena cava
ANSWER: A
EXPLANATION: The pulmonary veins carry oxygenated blood from the lungs back to the left atrium of the heart.

Q: Which gas is absorbed during photosynthesis?
A) Oxygen
B) Carbon dioxide
C) Nitrogen
D) Hydrogen
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: Plants absorb carbon dioxide from the atmosphere during the dark stage of photosynthesis.

Q: In a cross between two heterozygous tall pea plants (Tt × Tt), what is the expected ratio of tall to short offspring?
A) 1:1
B) 3:1
C) 2:1
D) 1:2:1
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: Using a Punnett square: TT, Tt, Tt, tt → 3 tall : 1 short.

Q: The genetic material found in chromosomes is made up of:
A) Protein and starch
B) DNA and protein
C) RNA and protein
D) Lipids and DNA
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: Chromosomes consist of DNA tightly coiled around histone proteins.

Q: A gene is best described as:
A) A sex cell
B) A unit of inheritance
C) A type of DNA
D) A type of RNA
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: A gene is a segment of DNA that codes for a particular trait and is the basic unit of inheritance.

Q: An ecosystem consists of:
A) Living organisms only
B) Non-living components only
C) Living organisms and their environment
D) All plants in an area
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: An ecosystem comprises biotic (living) and abiotic (non-living) components interacting together.

Q: The primary source of energy in an ecosystem is:
A) Plants
B) Sunlight
C) Herbivores
D) Soil
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: Sunlight is the ultimate source of energy that drives photosynthesis and therefore all food chains.

Q: In a food chain, energy flows from:
A) Consumers to producers
B) Decomposers to producers
C) Producers to consumers
D) Predators to prey
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: Energy flows in one direction: from producers (plants) to primary consumers and then to higher trophic levels.

Q: A group of the same species living in the same area is called a:
A) Community
B) Population
C) Habitat
D) Ecosystem
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: A population is a group of individuals of the same species occupying a particular area at the same time.

Q: The part of the brain responsible for balance and coordination is the:
A) Cerebrum
B) Medulla
C) Cerebellum
D) Thalamus
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: The cerebellum coordinates voluntary movements and maintains posture and balance.

Q: The hormone responsible for the regulation of blood sugar is:
A) Insulin
B) Adrenaline
C) Thyroxine
D) Estrogen
ANSWER: A
EXPLANATION: Insulin (produced by the pancreas) lowers blood glucose by promoting its uptake into cells.

Q: Which of these is a function of the liver?
A) Pumping blood
B) Filtration of blood
C) Production of bile
D) Production of insulin
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: The liver produces bile which emulsifies fats in the small intestine.

Q: Which blood cells are responsible for fighting infections?
A) Red blood cells
B) White blood cells
C) Platelets
D) Plasma
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: White blood cells (leucocytes) are the main cells of the immune system that destroy pathogens.

Q: The process by which plants lose water vapor through their leaves is called:
A) Transpiration
B) Respiration
C) Photosynthesis
D) Evaporation
ANSWER: A
EXPLANATION: Transpiration is the loss of water vapour mainly through the stomata of leaves.

Q: The structure that prevents food from entering the windpipe during swallowing is the:
A) Epiglottis
B) Trachea
C) Esophagus
D) Larynx
ANSWER: A
EXPLANATION: The epiglottis is a flap of cartilage that covers the glottis during swallowing, directing food into the oesophagus.

Q: The human heart has how many chambers?
A) Two
B) Three
C) Four
D) Five
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: The human heart has four chambers: two atria and two ventricles.

Q: Which macronutrient provides the most energy per gram?
A) Carbohydrates
B) Proteins
C) Fats
D) Vitamins
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: Fats yield approximately 9 kcal/g while carbohydrates and proteins yield about 4 kcal/g.

Q: The green pigment in plants responsible for photosynthesis is called:
A) Chlorophyll
B) Carotene
C) Xanthophyll
D) Anthocyanin
ANSWER: A
EXPLANATION: Chlorophyll absorbs light energy and is essential for the light-dependent reactions of photosynthesis.

Q: The site of protein synthesis in a cell is the:
A) Nucleus
B) Ribosome
C) Mitochondrion
D) Golgi apparatus
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: Ribosomes are the cellular organelles where amino acids are assembled into proteins (translation).

Q: Which organ in the human body produces insulin?
A) Liver
B) Pancreas
C) Kidney
D) Stomach
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: The islets of Langerhans in the pancreas secrete insulin.

Q: The type of reproduction that involves only one parent and produces offspring genetically identical to the parent is:
A) Sexual reproduction
B) Asexual reproduction
C) Fertilization
D) Pollination
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: Asexual reproduction (e.g. binary fission, budding, vegetative propagation) produces genetically identical offspring from a single parent.

Q: The smallest blood vessels in the body where exchange of substances occurs are called:
A) Arteries
B) Veins
C) Capillaries
D) Venules
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: Capillaries have thin walls that allow diffusion of oxygen, carbon dioxide, nutrients and waste products between blood and tissues.

Q: The main function of red blood cells is to:
A) Fight infection
B) Clot blood
C) Transport oxygen
D) Produce hormones
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: Red blood cells contain haemoglobin which binds and transports oxygen from the lungs to the body tissues.

Q: The enzyme responsible for breaking down starch in the mouth is:
A) Pepsin
B) Amylase
C) Lipase
D) Maltase
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: Salivary amylase (ptyalin) begins the digestion of starch into maltose in the mouth.

Q: The female reproductive part of a flower is called the:
A) Stamen
B) Pistil
C) Anther
D) Filament
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: The pistil (or carpel) consists of stigma, style and ovary and is the female reproductive organ of a flower.

Q: Which of these organisms is a decomposer?
A) Earthworm
B) Mushroom
C) Ant
D) Grass
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: Mushrooms (fungi) are classic decomposers that break down dead organic matter.

Q: Which part of the human brain controls voluntary movements?
A) Cerebrum
B) Cerebellum
C) Medulla
D) Hypothalamus
ANSWER: A
EXPLANATION: The cerebrum (especially the motor cortex) is responsible for initiating and controlling voluntary movements.
