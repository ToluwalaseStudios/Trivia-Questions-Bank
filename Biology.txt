BIOLOGY – 10 QUESTIONS WITH DETAILED EXPLANATIONS

Q1: Enzymes are
A) Inorganic catalysts
B) Non-sensitive to pH
C) Non-sensitive to temperature
D) Proteins
ANSWER: D
EXPLANATION: Enzymes are biological catalysts and are almost exclusively proteins (or RNA in the case of ribozymes). They are highly sensitive to both pH and temperature; extreme values denature them. Option A is therefore incorrect because enzymes are organic, not inorganic.

Q2: Which of the following undergoes Fehling’s test?
A) Starch
B) Protein
C) Glucose
D) Sucrose
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: Fehling’s test detects reducing sugars. Glucose is a reducing monosaccharide and gives a positive brick-red precipitate. Starch and sucrose are non-reducing; proteins do not contain free aldehyde or ketone groups that react with Fehling’s reagent.

Q3: Which of the following is the most advanced plant group?
A) Gymnosperm
B) Bryophytes
C) Thallophytes
D) Pteridophytes
ANSWER: A
EXPLANATION: Gymnosperms are seed-bearing vascular plants and are more advanced than the seedless vascular pteridophytes, the non-vascular bryophytes, and the simple thallophytes (algae and fungi). The presence of seeds and well-developed vascular tissue places gymnosperms at the highest evolutionary level among the given options.

Q4: Which of the following indicates a wide and narrow fluctuation in salinity respectively?
I. Eurybaric
II. Stenohaline
III. Euryhaline
A) I and II
B) II and III
C) III and II
D) III and I
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: Euryhaline organisms tolerate a wide range of salinity; stenohaline organisms tolerate only a narrow range. Eurybaric refers to pressure tolerance, not salinity. Therefore the correct pair for wide then narrow salinity fluctuation is III (euryhaline) and II (stenohaline).

Q5: BCG vaccine is used to treat ___
A) Tuberculosis
B) Asthma
C) Fibroid
D) Malaria
ANSWER: A
EXPLANATION: BCG (Bacillus Calmette–Guérin) is a live attenuated vaccine prepared from Mycobacterium bovis and is administered to protect against tuberculosis, especially severe forms in children. It has no therapeutic role in asthma, fibroids or malaria.

Q6: Which of the following is an abiotic instrument?
I. Thermometer
II. Secchi Disc
III. Wind vane
IV. Anemometer
A) I–IV
B) II–IV
C) I, II and IV
D) I only
ANSWER: A
EXPLANATION: All four instruments measure non-living (abiotic) environmental factors: temperature (thermometer), water transparency (Secchi disc), wind direction (wind vane) and wind speed (anemometer). None of them measure biotic components.

Q7: A plant growing on another plant without causing any harm is called?
A) Epiphyte
B) Xerophyte
C) Parasite
D) Holophyte
ANSWER: A
EXPLANATION: An epiphyte grows on another plant solely for physical support and obtains moisture and nutrients from the air or rain; it does not harm the host. A parasite derives nutrients at the host’s expense; a xerophyte is adapted to dry habitats; “holophyte” is not a standard ecological term.

Q8: The kind of competition that occurs between organisms of the same species is
A) Inter-specific competition
B) Intra-specific competition
C) Exploitation competition
D) Interference competition
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: Competition among individuals of the same species is called intra-specific competition. Inter-specific competition occurs between different species. Exploitation and interference are mechanisms of competition, not the taxonomic level at which it occurs.

Q9: The three domains of living organisms are?
A) Bacteria, fungi, eukaryotes
B) Archaea, fungi, bacteria
C) Prokaryotes, fungi, algae
D) Bacteria, Archaea, Eukaryota
ANSWER: D
EXPLANATION: The three-domain system of classification (Woese) divides all cellular life into Bacteria, Archaea and Eukaryota (Eukarya). Fungi and algae belong inside the domain Eukaryota; “prokaryotes” is a descriptive term, not a domain.

Q10: The pyrimidine found in RNA is
A) Uracil
B) Thymine
C) Cytosine
D) Adenine
ANSWER: A
EXPLANATION: RNA contains the pyrimidine bases cytosine and uracil. Thymine is the pyrimidine characteristic of DNA; adenine is a purine. Among the single options listed, uracil is the base that uniquely identifies RNA.
