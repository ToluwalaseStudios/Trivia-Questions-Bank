Q: The half life of a radioactive substance is 5 days. What percentage of it will remain after 20 days?
A) 6.25%
B) 12.5%
C) 25%
D) 42%
ANSWER: A
EXPLANATION: Number of half-lives = 20/5 = 4. After 4 half-lives the remaining amount is (1/2)^4 = 1/16 = 6.25%.

Q: Which of the following is true for a coin rolling down an inclined plane?
A) the motion of its centre is rotational while that of any other point is translational
B) the motion of the centre and that of any other point on the coin is translational
C) the motion of its centre is translational while that of any other point on the coin is rotational
D) the motion of its centre is translational while that of any other point on the coin is rotational
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: The centre of mass moves in a straight line (translational) while every other point rotates about the centre.

Q: The energy stored in an elastic material when a force of 1000 N causes it an extension of 1 cm is
A) 5 J
B) 10 J
C) 50 J
D) 100 J
ANSWER: A
EXPLANATION: Energy stored = ½ Fe = ½ × 1000 × 0.01 = 5 J.

Q: What is the reading on the Kelvin scale when the reading on the Fahrenheit scale is 188°F?
A) 359.7 K
B) 273 K
C) 90 K
D) 156 K
ANSWER: A
EXPLANATION: First convert to Celsius: C = (F – 32) × 5/9 = (188 – 32) × 5/9 = 86.67°C. Then K = C + 273 = 359.67 K ≈ 359.7 K.

Q: Given that M² L³ T⁻² is the dimension for G, the universal gravitational constant, the respective values of x, y & z are
A) 1, 3, –2
B) –1, –2 & 3
C) 2, –1 & 1
D) 2, 1 & –2
ANSWER: A
EXPLANATION: From Newton’s law F = G m₁ m₂ / r² → G = F r² / (m₁ m₂). Dimensions: [G] = [M L T⁻²] [L²] / [M²] = M⁻¹ L³ T⁻². Wait – standard is M⁻¹ L³ T⁻². The question states M² L³ T⁻² which appears mistyped in source; correct standard answer uses the known dimension.

Q: A load of 20 N on a wire of cross-sectional area 8 × 10⁻⁷ m² produces an extension of 10⁻⁴ m. Given that the length of the wire is 3 m, determine the Young’s modulus of the material.
A) 9 × 10⁻¹¹ N m⁻²
B) 8.5 × 10⁻¹¹ N m⁻²
C) 7.5 × 10¹¹ N m⁻²
D) 7 × 10⁻¹¹ N m⁻²
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: Y = (F/A) / (e/l) = (20 / 8×10⁻⁷) / (10⁻⁴ / 3) = (2.5 × 10⁷) / (3.33 × 10⁻⁵) ≈ 7.5 × 10¹¹ N m⁻².

Q: Provided that the amount of work required to move 500 µC of charge from point A to point B is 0.05 J, the potential difference between A and B is
A) 100 V
B) 50 V
C) 200 V
D) 500 V
ANSWER: A
EXPLANATION: V = W / Q = 0.05 / (500 × 10⁻⁶) = 0.05 / 0.0005 = 100 V.

Q: Which of the following has the shortest wavelength?
A) Infra-red rays
B) Ultraviolet rays
C) Radio waves
D) Gamma rays
ANSWER: D
EXPLANATION: Gamma rays have the highest frequency and therefore the shortest wavelength in the electromagnetic spectrum.

Q: Two 10 Ω resistors are connected in parallel. What is the combined resistance?
A) 10 Ω
B) 20 Ω
C) 5 Ω
D) 75 Ω
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: 1/R = 1/10 + 1/10 = 2/10 → R = 5 Ω.

Q: A 14 kg body is released from rest on a smooth inclined plane. What is the acceleration of the body down the plane, given that the angle between the plane and the horizontal is 60°? (g = 10 m s⁻²)
A) 10 m s⁻²
B) 5 m s⁻²
C) 8.66 m s⁻²
D) 4.33 m s⁻²
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: a = g sin θ = 10 × sin 60° = 10 × (√3/2) ≈ 8.66 m s⁻².
