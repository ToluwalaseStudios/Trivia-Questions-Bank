Q: A stone is thrown vertically upward with an initial velocity of 20 m/s. Taking g = 10 m/s², how long does it take to return to its point of projection?
A) 1 s
B) 2 s
C) 4 s
D) 5 s
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: Time to reach max height = u/g = 20/10 = 2 s. Total time up and down = 2 × 2 = 4 s.

Q: A ray of light travelling in glass (refractive index 1.5) strikes the glass-air boundary at an angle greater than the critical angle. What happens to the ray?
A) It refracts into air
B) It undergoes total internal reflection
C) It is absorbed
D) It splits into two rays
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: When the angle of incidence exceeds the critical angle in a denser medium, no light escapes into the rarer medium; the ray is entirely reflected back (total internal reflection).

Q: Two resistors of 4 Ω and 6 Ω are connected in parallel. What is the effective resistance?
A) 10 Ω
B) 2.4 Ω
C) 1.5 Ω
D) 24 Ω
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: 1/R = 1/4 + 1/6 = 3/12 + 2/12 = 5/12, so R = 12/5 = 2.4 Ω.

Q: A wire of length L and cross-sectional area A has a Young's modulus E. If the length is doubled and area halved, how does the extension for the same applied force change?
A) Doubles
B) Quadruples
C) Halves
D) Remains the same
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: Extension e = FL/(AE). Doubling L doubles e; halving A doubles e again, so e increases fourfold overall.

Q: A body of mass 2 kg moving at 3 m/s collides perfectly inelastically with a stationary body of mass 4 kg. What is their common velocity after collision?
A) 0.5 m/s
B) 1.0 m/s
C) 1.5 m/s
D) 2.0 m/s
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: By conservation of momentum: (2×3) + (4×0) = (2+4)v → 6 = 6v → v = 1.0 m/s.

Q: The half-life of a radioactive substance is 20 minutes. What fraction of the original sample remains after 1 hour?
A) 1/2
B) 1/4
C) 1/8
D) 1/16
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: 1 hour = 3 half-lives (60/20). Remaining fraction = (1/2)^3 = 1/8.

Q: A concave mirror of focal length 15 cm forms a real image at 30 cm from the mirror. What is the object distance?
A) 10 cm
B) 15 cm
C) 30 cm
D) 45 cm
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: Using 1/f = 1/u + 1/v: 1/15 = 1/u + 1/30 → 1/u = 1/15 - 1/30 = 1/30, so u = 30 cm.

Q: Which of the following correctly relates the wavelength (λ), frequency (f), and velocity (v) of a wave?
A) v = λ/f
B) v = λf
C) v = f/λ
D) λ = vf
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: The wave equation states velocity equals wavelength multiplied by frequency: v = λf.

Q: A transformer has 200 turns on the primary coil and 50 turns on the secondary. If the primary voltage is 240 V, what is the secondary voltage?
A) 960 V
B) 480 V
C) 60 V
D) 30 V
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: Vs/Vp = Ns/Np → Vs = 240 × (50/200) = 240 × 0.25 = 60 V.

Q: An object of weight 50 N is completely immersed in water and experiences an upthrust of 20 N. What is its apparent weight in water?
A) 20 N
B) 30 N
C) 50 N
D) 70 N
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: Apparent weight = actual weight − upthrust = 50 − 20 = 30 N.
